# Cinematic asset requirements

No new assets were generated for this change. The list below is what a real hero video would need. Do not replace it with unrelated stock footage.

| Asset | Purpose | Orientation | Aspect ratio | Resolution | Subject and composition | Lighting and mood | Transparency | Mobile version |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero loop | Ambient motion behind the headline | Landscape | 16:9 | 1920x1080, H.264 and AV1, under 2 MB | A calm, unbranded interior of a home office or kitchen table with money papers and a warm lamp; no visible text, logos or faces in close-up | Late-afternoon warm light, slow camera drift | No | 9:16 crop, 1080x1920, under 1.2 MB |
| Hero poster | Shown before video loads | Landscape | 16:9 | 1600x900 WebP | First frame of the loop | Same as loop | No | Portrait poster 1080x1350 |
| Story stills (3) | Support scene 4 | Portrait | 4:5 | 1200x1500 WebP | One object each: a budget notebook, a credit card, a house key on a table | Soft daylight, neutral background | No | Same |
| Product detail crop | Comparison section | Square | 1:1 | 800x800 WebP | Clean close-up of a calculator screen with no real brand names | Neutral | No | Same |

## Rules for any asset
- No fake text, no fake logos, no fake people presented as real customers, and no invented business claims.
- Unrelated stock imagery is not allowed on the homepage.
- Each asset must have a defined focal point for mobile crops.
- Video must be muted, playsinline, lazy loaded below the fold, and have a poster.
