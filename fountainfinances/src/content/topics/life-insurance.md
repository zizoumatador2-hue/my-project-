## Term vs. permanent life insurance

| | Term life | Permanent (whole/universal) |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage length | A set term (e.g., 10–30 years) | Lifetime, if premiums are paid |
| Cost | Lower | Much higher for the same death benefit |
| Cash value | None | Builds cash value over time |
| Best for | Income replacement while dependents rely on you | Lifelong needs, certain estate planning goals |

## How much coverage do you need?

A simple approach adds up what your family would need if you died:

1. **Income replacement** for the years your dependents would rely on it.
2. **Debts** such as the mortgage and loans.
3. **Future goals** like children’s education.
4. **Final expenses.**

Then subtract existing savings, investments and other coverage. Many rules of thumb suggest several times your annual income, but a needs-based estimate is more accurate.

## Buying a policy

Compare quotes for the same coverage amount and term, check the insurer’s financial strength ratings, and confirm the company and agent are licensed with your state insurance department.
