## Get ready before you shop

Buying your first home goes more smoothly when your finances are prepared months in advance:

- **Check your credit reports** and fix errors.
- **Lower your debt-to-income ratio** by paying down balances.
- **Save for more than the down payment:** closing costs, moving, furniture and repairs.
- **Keep your emergency fund intact** after closing.
- **Avoid new credit** applications during the process.

## Loan options popular with first-time buyers

- **Conventional loans** with low-down-payment programs from Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac.
- **FHA loans**, which allow 3.5% down with a credit score of at least 580.
- **VA loans** for eligible service members and veterans, often with no down payment.
- **USDA loans** for eligible buyers in qualifying rural and suburban areas.

## First-time home buyer programs

Your **state housing finance agency** is often the best starting point for down payment and closing cost assistance, below-market-rate mortgages and homebuyer education. The CFPB’s [housing counselor search](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/find-a-housing-counselor/) lists HUD-approved agencies that provide free or low-cost guidance.
