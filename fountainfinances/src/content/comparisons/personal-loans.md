## How to compare personal loan offers

1. **Prequalify with several lenders** that use soft inquiries.
2. **Compare APRs** for the same loan amount and term.
3. **Calculate total cost:** monthly payment × number of payments.
4. **Check the fee structure**: an origination fee reduces the cash you receive.
5. **Look for flexibility:** autopay discounts, due-date changes and hardship options.

## Worked example: a lower rate with a fee vs. a higher rate without one

Say you need $10,000 in hand and are choosing between two hypothetical 36-month offers:

| | Offer A | Offer B |
|---|---|---|
| Interest rate | 11.99% | 9.99% |
| Origination fee | None | 5%, deducted from the proceeds |
| Amount borrowed to receive $10,000 | $10,000 | $10,526.32 ($10,000 ÷ 0.95) |
| Monthly payment | $332.10 | $339.61 |
| Total repaid | $11,955.43 | $12,225.79 |
| Cost of borrowing | $1,955.43 | $2,225.79 |
| APR on the $10,000 you receive | 11.99% | About 13.55% |

Offer B advertises the lower rate but costs about $270 more, because the fee is charged up front and you pay interest on it. The APR captures this, which is why the Truth in Lending Act requires lenders to disclose it and why it is the better number to compare.
