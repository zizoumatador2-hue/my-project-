## How to choose among these accounts

Most high-yield savings accounts look similar at first glance. The differences that matter are how consistently the bank keeps its rate competitive, how easily you can move money, and whether the account fits the rest of your banking.

- **Want everything in one place?** Choose a bank that also offers checking, so transfers are instant.
- **Need ATM access to savings?** Few online savings accounts offer it; look for an optional ATM card.
- **Saving for several goals?** Buckets or vaults inside one account keep goals organized.
- **Qualifying activity required?** Some accounts pay their top rate only with direct deposit or other conditions — read the rules.

## Before you open an account

1. Confirm the bank’s FDIC status on the FDIC’s BankFind tool.
2. Check today’s APY on the bank’s own site and read the rate conditions.
3. Link your checking account and set up an automatic transfer.
