## Matching an account to how you bank

- **Handle cash often?** A bank with branches or partner cash-deposit locations will be easier than an online-only bank that doesn’t accept cash.
- **Travel or use many ATMs?** ATM fee rebates can matter more than any other feature.
- **Mostly digital?** Online banks usually have fewer fees and strong apps.
- **Prefer member ownership?** Credit unions offer checking with NCUA insurance; membership requirements apply.

## Switching checklist

1. Open the new account and order a debit card.
2. Update direct deposit with your employer.
3. Move automatic bill payments and subscriptions.
4. Keep the old account open with a small buffer for one or two statement cycles.
5. Close the old account once everything has moved.
