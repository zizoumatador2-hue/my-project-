**Quick answer:** To choose a car dealership near you in Alabama, compare reviews, confirm the dealer's address and hours, ask for a written out-the-door price, make sure they allow an independent inspection, and consider how easy it will be to get help after the sale.

## 1. Start with nearby dealers

Proximity matters for test drives, paperwork and follow-up service. On BamaMotors you can open the [Alabama dealer directory](/dealers), enter your ZIP code or tap **Use my location**, and see dealers sorted by distance.

## 2. Read reviews — and responses

Look for patterns, not one-off complaints. Pay attention to how the dealer responds to criticism. BamaMotors only shows reviews from signed-in shoppers after moderation.

## 3. Demand pricing transparency

Ask for an **itemized out-the-door price** in writing before you visit. A dealer who won't provide one is a red flag.

## 4. Confirm inspection access

A confident dealer will let you take the car to an independent mechanic. If they refuse, walk away.

## 5. Understand the warranty

The federal Buyers Guide on the window tells you whether a vehicle is sold *as-is* or with a warranty. Ask what's covered and for how long.

## 6. Consider after-sale support

Does the dealer have a service department? Will they help with title and registration? These details matter after you drive away.
