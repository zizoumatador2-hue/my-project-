**Quick answer:** When you buy a used car from a private seller in Alabama, verify that the seller's name matches the title, check the VIN for title brands and recalls, get a signed title and a bill of sale, pay with a traceable method, and register the car at your county license office within the required time.

Private sellers often ask less than dealers, but there's no dealer handling the paperwork, no Buyers Guide, and almost always no warranty. Follow these steps to do it right.

## 1. Verify the seller and the title

- The name on the seller's **photo ID should match the title**.
- Check for a **lienholder** listed on the title. If there's an unpaid loan, the lien must be released before ownership can transfer.
- Run the VIN through [NMVTIS](https://vehiclehistory.bja.ojp.gov/) for salvage, rebuilt or flood brands, and check open recalls at [NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).

## 2. Inspect and test drive

Private sales are almost always **as-is**. Pay an independent mechanic for a pre-purchase inspection. Use our [inspection checklist](/blog/how-to-inspect-a-used-car-alabama) and [test drive checklist](/blog/test-drive-checklist).

## 3. Write a bill of sale

A bill of sale protects both buyer and seller. Include:

| Item | Example |
|---|---|
| Date of sale | October 6, 2026 |
| Buyer and seller names and addresses | Full legal names |
| Vehicle year, make, model | 2016 Honda Accord EX |
| VIN | All 17 characters |
| Odometer reading | As shown at sale |
| Sale price | Exact amount paid |
| Signatures | Both parties |

## 4. Pay safely

Avoid carrying large amounts of cash. A **cashier's check** obtained at the seller's bank (or completed together at your bank) is traceable and hard to fake. Never wire money or pay a deposit for a car you haven't seen.

## 5. Complete the title transfer

The seller signs the title over to you, recording the odometer reading where required. Check that the VIN on the title matches the car's dashboard and door-jamb VIN.

## 6. Register and pay taxes

Take the signed title, bill of sale, proof of insurance and your ID to your **county license office**. Alabama generally requires registration within **20 calendar days** of purchase. The buyer typically pays the applicable sales or use tax at registration. See the [Alabama Department of Revenue](https://www.revenue.alabama.gov/motor-vehicle/) for current rules.
