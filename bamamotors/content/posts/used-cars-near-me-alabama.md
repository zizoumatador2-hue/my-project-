**Quick answer:** To find used cars near you in Alabama, search by your ZIP code with a 25–50 mile radius, sort by distance, then compare prices on the same model across nearby dealers. Call or message the dealer to confirm the car is still available before you drive over.

"Used cars near me" is one of the most common car searches in America — and for good reason. A car that's 15 minutes away is easier to test drive, inspect and service than one three states over. Here's how to make local shopping work in your favor.

## Step 1: Search by ZIP code, not just city

City names can be misleading — a dealer with a Birmingham mailing address might be in Hoover, Bessemer or Trussville. On BamaMotors you can [search used cars by ZIP code](/used-cars) and choose a radius of 10, 25, 50, 100 or 200 miles. Choose **Closest first** in the sort menu to see the nearest vehicles at the top.

On your phone, tap **Use my location** and we'll find the nearest Alabama ZIP code for you. Your exact location is never stored.

## Step 2: Pick the right radius for where you live

| Where you live | Suggested radius | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Birmingham, Huntsville, Mobile, Montgomery | 25 miles | Plenty of dealers close by |
| Smaller cities (Dothan, Gadsden, Florence) | 50 miles | Fewer dealers, more spread out |
| Rural Alabama | 100 miles | Widen the net, then narrow down |

## Step 3: Compare the same car at nearby dealers

Once you have a shortlist, compare the same make and model across dealers. Look at:

- **Asking price** and what's included
- **Mileage** — a few thousand miles can matter more than a few hundred dollars
- **Vehicle history** — title status, accidents and number of owners
- **Photos** — more photos usually means a more transparent seller

Our city pages, like [used cars in Birmingham](/used-cars/birmingham-al) and [used cars in Huntsville](/used-cars/huntsville-al), include a live price table so you can see what's normal in your area.

## Step 4: Confirm before you drive

Inventory moves quickly. Before you go, use **Contact Dealer** or **Request Price** to confirm the car is still available and ask for an itemized out-the-door price. That one message can save you an afternoon.

## Step 5: Check the dealer, too

Distance isn't everything. Read the dealer's reviews, confirm their address and hours, and make sure they'll let you get an [independent inspection](/blog/how-to-inspect-a-used-car-alabama). Our guide to [choosing a car dealership in Alabama](/blog/choosing-a-car-dealership-in-alabama) has a full checklist.
