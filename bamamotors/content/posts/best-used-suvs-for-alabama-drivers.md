**Quick answer:** The best used SUV for Alabama drivers is one that fits your daily driving and budget: a compact SUV for commuting and fuel economy, a midsize two-row SUV for extra cargo space, or a three-row SUV for larger families. Prioritize service records, strong A/C, good tires and a clean title over brand or badge.

SUVs are one of the most popular vehicle types on Alabama roads. Start by narrowing down the size you need.

## Compact SUVs

Popular compact SUVs include the **Toyota RAV4**, **Honda CR-V**, **Mazda CX-5**, **Hyundai Tucson**, **Kia Sportage**, **Nissan Rogue**, **Ford Escape** and **Chevrolet Equinox**. They're easy to park, typically more fuel-efficient than larger SUVs, and a good fit for commuters on I-65 or I-20.

**What to check:** CVT behavior on models that use one (smooth, no shudder), A/C performance, and tire wear that could signal alignment issues.

## Midsize two-row SUVs

Options such as the **Ford Edge**, **Nissan Murano**, **Honda Passport** and **Jeep Grand Cherokee** add cargo space and power without a third row.

**What to check:** Suspension noise over bumps, towing history if the vehicle has a hitch, and infotainment function.

## Three-row SUVs

Families often shop the **Toyota Highlander**, **Honda Pilot**, **Kia Telluride**, **Hyundai Palisade**, **Chevrolet Traverse**, **Ford Explorer** and full-size models like the **Chevrolet Tahoe** and **Ford Expedition**.

**What to check:** Rear A/C (a must in Alabama summers), third-row seat mechanisms, sliding-door or power-liftgate operation, and interior wear.

## Before you buy

Check open recalls with the VIN at [NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls), review the vehicle history, and get an independent inspection. See the [used car inspection checklist](/blog/how-to-inspect-a-used-car-alabama).
