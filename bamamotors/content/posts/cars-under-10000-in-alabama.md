**Quick answer:** Under $10,000 in Alabama you'll typically find older, higher-mileage sedans, hatchbacks and some SUVs and trucks. Focus on condition, service records and a clean title rather than age or brand, and always budget for an inspection and a few immediate maintenance items.

## What $10,000 buys

In this range, the most value often comes from **compact and midsize sedans** that were well maintained. Trucks and SUVs hold their value, so you'll see higher mileage for the same money. See what's available right now in [cars under $10,000](/used-cars/under-10000).

## What $5,000 buys

Under $5,000, expect older vehicles with significant mileage. These can still make sense as a first car or a second vehicle, especially if you can pay cash. Browse [cars under $5,000](/used-cars/under-5000).

## Red flags on budget cars

- **Title brands:** Salvage, rebuilt or flood titles lower value and can affect insurance.
- **Warning lights** on the dashboard.
- **No service records** at all.
- **Fresh undercoating** or new carpet in an old car, which can hide rust or flood damage.
- **Pressure to pay a deposit** before you've seen or inspected the car.

## Budget checklist

1. Leave 10–15% of your budget for tax, title, registration and immediate maintenance.
2. Check the title history and open recalls using the VIN.
3. Get an independent inspection. It's the best $100–$200 you'll spend.
4. Compare out-the-door prices across dealers.
