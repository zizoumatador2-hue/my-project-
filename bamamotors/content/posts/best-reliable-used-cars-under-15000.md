**Quick answer:** The most reliable used car under $15,000 is one with a proven model reputation **and** a documented maintenance history. Compact and midsize sedans and small crossovers from brands known for durability are a good starting point, but always research the exact year and engine, check recalls, and get an inspection.

## Start with vehicle types that age well

In this price range, shoppers often find good value in:

- **Compact sedans and hatchbacks:** Toyota Corolla, Honda Civic, Mazda3
- **Midsize sedans:** Toyota Camry, Honda Accord
- **Small crossovers:** Honda CR-V, Toyota RAV4, Mazda CX-5 (often older or higher-mileage at this budget)

These models have long production runs, widely available parts and many independent mechanics who know them well. That said, every model has strong and weak years. Research the specific year.

## What to prioritize under $15,000

1. **Service records** over low mileage alone.
2. **A clean title:** Verify through [NMVTIS](https://vehiclehistory.bja.ojp.gov/).
3. **No open recalls:** Check the VIN at [NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
4. **Simple drivetrains:** Fewer complex systems mean fewer expensive surprises.
5. **Good tires and brakes:** Or budget to replace them.

## Research checklist for a specific car

| Question | Where to look |
|---|---|
| Known problems for this year/engine? | Owner forums, NHTSA complaints |
| Recalls completed? | NHTSA recall lookup, dealer records |
| Typical repair costs? | Independent mechanic, repair-cost estimators |
| Insurance cost? | Quote from your insurer using the VIN |
