**Quick answer:** A used car is usually the better value because the first owner absorbs the steepest depreciation, lowering your purchase price, taxes and often insurance. A new car makes sense if you want a full factory warranty, the latest safety tech and promotional financing rates, and plan to keep the car for many years.

## Side-by-side comparison

| Factor | New car | Used car |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase price | Highest | Lower for the same model |
| Depreciation | Steepest in the first years | Slower — the first owner took the biggest hit |
| Sales tax | Based on the higher price | Based on a lower price |
| Insurance | Often higher | Often lower |
| Financing rates | Promotional rates sometimes available | Usually somewhat higher |
| Warranty | Full factory warranty | Remaining factory warranty, CPO or as-is |
| Selection | Current model year only | Many years, trims and colors |

## When used makes more sense

- You want the most car for your money.
- You're buying a first car or a second vehicle.
- You're comfortable doing an inspection and checking history.

## When new makes more sense

- You plan to keep the vehicle for a decade.
- You value a full warranty and the newest safety features.
- A manufacturer is offering low-rate financing.

## The middle ground: certified pre-owned

Certified pre-owned (CPO) vehicles are late-model used cars that meet a manufacturer or dealer inspection standard and typically include extended warranty coverage. Use the **Condition** filter on BamaMotors to find CPO listings.
