**Quick answer:** On a used car test drive, start the engine cold, confirm every warning light turns off, drive for at least 20 minutes on city streets and the highway, brake firmly, test the A/C and every electronic feature, and check underneath for leaks afterward. Walk away if the seller won't allow an independent inspection.

## Before you start the engine

1. **Cold start:** Ask the seller not to warm it up.
2. **Warning lights:** All should illuminate, then go out.
3. **Seats, mirrors and visibility:** Can you get comfortable?
4. **Tire tread and age:** Check the DOT date code.
5. **Smell:** Musty or heavily perfumed interiors can hide water damage.

## In the city

6. **Smooth shifting:** No clunks, slips or hesitation.
7. **Steering:** No play, pulling or noises at full lock.
8. **Bumps:** Listen for clunks or rattles from the suspension.
9. **Low-speed braking:** No grinding or squealing.
10. **A/C and heat:** Full blast for several minutes (essential in Alabama).

## On the highway

11. **Merging:** Strong, steady acceleration.
12. **Tracking:** The car should go straight with a light hand on the wheel.
13. **Vibration:** At 55–70 mph, the steering wheel should stay steady.
14. **Cruise control** and driver-assist features.
15. **Noise:** Excessive wind or tire noise.

## Braking test

16. **Firm stop from speed** in a safe, empty area: no pulling or pulsing.

## Electronics

17. **Windows, locks, mirrors** and **all lights**.
18. **Infotainment, Bluetooth, USB** and backup camera.

## After the drive

19. **Look underneath** for fresh drips.
20. **Under the hood:** Smells of burning oil or sweet coolant are warning signs.
