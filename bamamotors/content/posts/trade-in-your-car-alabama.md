**Quick answer:** To get the most for your trade-in, check its value from several sources, clean it and gather service records, fix small issues, and negotiate the trade-in separately from the price of the car you're buying. In Alabama, a trade-in generally reduces the taxable amount of your dealer purchase.

## Know your car's value first

Look up your car on several pricing guides and compare similar listings near you. Be honest about condition — "good" and "excellent" can mean very different numbers.

## Prepare your car

- **Clean it inside and out.** First impressions affect appraisals.
- **Gather records.** Service history shows the car was cared for.
- **Fix the cheap stuff.** Burned-out bulbs, wiper blades and low fluids are inexpensive to fix.
- **Find both keys.** A missing key can reduce your offer.

## Negotiate in the right order

1. Agree on the **price of the car you're buying**.
2. Then negotiate the **trade-in value**.
3. Then discuss **financing**.

Keeping these separate makes it harder for numbers to shift around.

## Trade-in vs. private sale

| | Trade-in | Private sale |
|---|---|---|
| Typical value | Lower | Higher |
| Time and effort | Minimal | Listing, showing, paperwork |
| Sales tax effect | Can reduce taxable price | None on your next purchase |
| Risk | Low | Scams, no-shows, payment issues |

## Paying off a loan on your trade-in

If you still owe money, the dealer pays off your loan and applies any remaining equity to your purchase. If you owe more than the car is worth, the difference ("negative equity") may be added to your new loan — be careful, as that increases what you finance.
