**Quick answer:** Alabama requires at least 25/50/25 liability insurance — $25,000 bodily injury per person, $50,000 per accident and $25,000 property damage. Have coverage in place before you drive a newly purchased used car, and bring proof of insurance when you register it at your county office.

Insurance is one of the costs shoppers forget when they budget for a used car. Here's what Alabama requires and what's smart to add.

## The legal minimum: 25/50/25

Alabama's mandatory liability insurance law requires:

| Coverage | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Bodily injury liability (per person) | $25,000 |
| Bodily injury liability (per accident) | $50,000 |
| Property damage liability | $25,000 |

Liability insurance pays for damage you cause to others. It does **not** pay to repair your own car.

## Coverage worth considering

- **Collision** — pays to repair your car after a crash.
- **Comprehensive** — covers theft, hail, flooding and falling trees. Valuable in Alabama's storm seasons.
- **Uninsured/underinsured motorist** — protects you if the other driver has little or no insurance.
- **Gap insurance** — on a financed car, covers the difference between the loan balance and the car's value if it's totaled.

If you finance your purchase, your lender will likely require collision and comprehensive coverage. See [how to finance a used car in Alabama](/blog/how-to-finance-a-used-car-in-alabama).

## When you need proof of insurance

1. **Before you drive away** — call your insurer or use their app to add the car using its VIN.
2. **At registration** — bring proof of insurance to your county license office. Alabama generally requires registration within 20 calendar days of purchase.
3. **Any time you drive** — keep your insurance card in the car or on your phone.

## How the car you buy affects your premium

Insurance costs vary with the vehicle's value, repair costs, safety ratings and theft rates — plus your age, driving record and ZIP code. Before you commit to a car, get a quick insurance quote using its VIN. Two cars with the same price can have very different premiums.
