**Quick answer:** To list inventory on BamaMotors, create a dealer account, complete your dealership profile, add each vehicle with accurate details and plenty of photos, and respond to leads quickly from your dashboard. Use featured listings to highlight priority vehicles.

## 1. Create your dealer account

Go to [List Your Dealership](/for-dealers) and sign up with your dealership details. Our team reviews new dealer accounts before listings go public.

## 2. Complete your profile

Add your logo, description, hours, website and Alabama dealer license number. A complete profile builds trust and helps shoppers find you in local search.

## 3. Add vehicles

For each vehicle, enter the year, make, model, trim, price, mileage, VIN and specs. Be honest in the vehicle history section — it saves time with serious buyers.

### Photo tips

- Shoot in open shade or on an overcast day.
- Include front 3/4, rear 3/4, both sides, interior, dashboard, odometer and cargo area.
- Show imperfections honestly.

## 4. Respond to leads fast

Speed matters. Leads arrive in your dashboard and by email. Update the status as you work each lead: New → Contacted → Qualified → Converted or Closed.

## 5. Feature your best vehicles

Featured vehicles appear first in search results and on the homepage. Basic and Pro plans include featured slots.
