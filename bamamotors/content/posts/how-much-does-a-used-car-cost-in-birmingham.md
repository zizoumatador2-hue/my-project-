**Quick answer:** The cost of a used car in Birmingham is the dealer's asking price plus Alabama's 2% state automotive sales tax, local county and city taxes, title and registration fees, and the dealer's documentation fee. For current asking prices by body type, check the live price table on our [Birmingham used cars page](/used-cars/birmingham-al).

Used car prices move constantly with supply, interest rates and the season. Rather than quote numbers that go stale, BamaMotors publishes **live price ranges** calculated from active listings. This guide explains everything that goes into your final number.

## The asking price

Asking prices in the Birmingham metro depend on age, mileage, condition and body type. Trucks and three-row SUVs typically cost more than compact sedans of the same age. To see today's prices, open the [Birmingham page](/used-cars/birmingham-al) and look at the price table. It shows the lowest, average and highest asking prices for each body type, updated in real time. You can also look at [used trucks in Birmingham](/used-cars/birmingham-al/trucks) and [used SUVs in Birmingham](/used-cars/birmingham-al/suvs).

## Taxes

Alabama charges a **2% state automotive sales tax**, and counties and cities add their own local rates. Your combined rate depends on where you live, not necessarily where you buy. The [Alabama Department of Revenue](https://www.revenue.alabama.gov/) publishes current local rates.

## Title, registration and tag

After purchase you'll pay title and registration fees at your county license office. Jefferson County has offices in Birmingham and Bessemer. Registration costs vary by vehicle type, and some counties add local fees.

## Dealer fees

Most dealers charge a **documentation fee** for processing paperwork. It varies from dealer to dealer, which is why comparing **out-the-door prices** is essential. Watch for optional add-ons such as paint protection, VIN etching or extended service contracts. You can decline them.

## How to pay less

- Compare the same model across several dealers using the **Lowest price** sort.
- Be flexible on color and trim.
- Get pre-approved so you can compare financing offers. See [how to finance a used car in Alabama](/blog/how-to-finance-a-used-car-in-alabama).
- Use **Request Price** on a listing to get an itemized quote before you visit.
