A cruise can be a surprisingly romantic way to see the Caribbean. You unpack once, wake up in a new port and spend evenings at sea watching the sunset from your balcony.

## Tips for couples

- **Choose the ship for its vibe.** Some ships are packed with family attractions; others focus on dining, spas and quiet decks. Several lines have adults-only areas or are adults-only entirely.
- **Book a balcony.** For many couples, a private balcony is the single best cabin upgrade.
- **Pair it with a beach stay.** Add a few nights in [Miami](/destinations/miami/) or the [Florida Keys](/destinations/florida/) before or after sailing.

Many Caribbean itineraries call at the [Bahamas](/destinations/bahamas/), [Jamaica](/destinations/jamaica/) and Mexico's Cozumel. Explore [Caribbean vacations for couples](/guides/caribbean-vacations-for-couples/) to see which islands you might want to return to for a longer stay.
