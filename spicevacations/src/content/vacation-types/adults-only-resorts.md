Adults-only resorts are one of the simplest ways to guarantee a calmer vacation. Without kids' clubs and splash pools, the energy shifts toward spas, quiet beaches, cocktail bars and slower dinners.

## What to look for

- **Age policy.** Minimum ages differ, often 16 or 18.
- **Meal plan.** All-inclusive, room-only, or breakfast-included.
- **Room location.** Swim-up and oceanfront rooms book out first.
- **Atmosphere.** Some adults-only resorts are lively with DJs and pool parties; others are serene.

## Why choose an adults-only resort

Adults-only resorts set a minimum guest age, usually 16 or 18 depending on the property, so pools, restaurants and beaches stay quieter. They suit travelers who want to read by the pool and linger over dinner. Most emphasize spas, dining and calm beaches.

## Questions to ask before booking

1. **What is the minimum age?** It varies by resort.
2. **Is it all-inclusive or room-only?** Adults-only resorts come in both styles, and the price difference can be large.
3. **Which restaurants need reservations?** At larger all-inclusive resorts, specialty restaurants can book up days ahead.
4. **How far is the airport?** Transfers in the Riviera Maya or Negril can take 60 to 90 minutes or more.
5. **What is the beach like?** Some Caribbean beaches see seasonal sargassum seaweed, usually heaviest from spring through summer. Ask how the resort manages it.
