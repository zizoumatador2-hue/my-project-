Adults-only resorts are one of the simplest ways to guarantee a calmer vacation. Without kids' clubs and splash pools, the energy shifts toward spas, quiet beaches, cocktail bars and slower dinners.

## What to look for

- **Age policy.** Minimum ages differ, often 16 or 18.
- **Meal plan.** All-inclusive, room-only, or breakfast-included.
- **Room location.** Swim-up and oceanfront rooms book out first.
- **Atmosphere.** Some adults-only resorts are lively with DJs and pool parties; others are serene.
