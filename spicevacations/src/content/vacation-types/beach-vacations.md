Few things beat a beach vacation for pure relaxation. The key is matching the beach to what you want: calm, clear water for snorkeling; big surf for watching; or a lively strip with restaurants and bars.

Explore [best beach vacations](/guides/best-beach-vacations/), [best beach vacations in the USA](/guides/best-beach-vacations-in-the-usa/) and [best beach vacations in the Caribbean](/guides/best-beach-vacations-in-the-caribbean/).

## How to choose the right beach

- **For swimming and snorkeling**, look for sheltered bays and reef-protected coasts: Florida's Gulf Coast, parts of the Riviera Maya, Negril's Seven Mile Beach and many Caribbean islands.
- **For drama and scenery**, Hawaii's volcanic coastlines and St. Lucia's Pitons are hard to beat. Some of the most beautiful beaches have strong currents, so swim where lifeguards and local advice say it is safe.
- **For an easy trip with no passport**, Florida, Hawaii, Southern California and the Carolinas give you warm water without international travel. U.S. citizens visiting Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands do not need a passport either.

## Timing your beach vacation

Caribbean and Mexico beaches are busiest and most expensive from mid-December through April. Late spring and early summer often bring better rates, and the Atlantic hurricane season (June 1 to November 30) is the trade-off later in the year. In Caribbean Mexico and parts of the Caribbean, seaweed (sargassum) can arrive in warmer months, so check recent reports before you book a specific beach. Hawaii is a year-round destination, with bigger winter surf on north-facing shores.
