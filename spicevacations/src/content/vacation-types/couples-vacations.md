The right couples vacation is the one that makes both of you exhale. Some couples want seven days of doing nothing on a quiet beach; others want hiking in the morning and a tasting menu at night. The trips on SpiceVacations are organized so you can find your overlap quickly.

## Popular couples vacation styles

1. **All-inclusive beach resorts** in Mexico, Jamaica, the Dominican Republic and the Bahamas, where meals and drinks are covered. See [all-inclusive vacations for couples](/guides/all-inclusive-vacations-for-couples/).
2. **Island adventures** in Hawaii, mixing snorkeling, volcano landscapes and sunset dinners. Read our [Hawaii couples vacation](/guides/hawaii-couples-vacation/) guide.
3. **City breaks** with great food and nightlife, like a [Las Vegas couples vacation](/guides/las-vegas-couples-vacation/) or a Miami weekend.
4. **Budget-smart trips** that still feel special. See [best couples vacations under $2,000](/guides/best-couples-vacations-under-2000/).
