An all-inclusive resort takes the math out of your vacation. Once you have paid, meals, snacks and drinks are covered, and many resorts also include non-motorized water sports, fitness classes and nightly entertainment.

## How to choose an all-inclusive

1. **Adults-only or family?** Couples usually prefer adults-only properties for a calmer pace.
2. **Restaurant variety.** For a week-long stay, look for several à la carte restaurants.
3. **Beach quality.** Some coastlines see seasonal sargassum seaweed; ask about the resort's beach cleanup.
4. **Transfer time.** An hour or less from the airport keeps travel days easy.
