A honeymoon is often the first big trip you plan as a married couple, and it happens right after months of wedding logistics. The best honeymoon vacations remove decisions rather than adding them: easy transfers, excellent service, and a room you actually want to spend time in.

## Where couples go

- **Hawaii** for dramatic landscapes and no passport requirement for U.S. citizens. See our [Hawaii couples vacation](/guides/hawaii-couples-vacation/) guide.
- **Mexico's Riviera Maya and Los Cabos** for adults-only all-inclusive resorts. See [best adults-only resorts in Mexico](/guides/best-adults-only-resorts-in-mexico/).
- **The Caribbean**, from St. Lucia's Pitons to Jamaica's couples-only resorts. Read [Caribbean vacations for couples](/guides/caribbean-vacations-for-couples/).
