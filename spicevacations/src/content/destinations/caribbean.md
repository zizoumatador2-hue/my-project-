The Caribbean is not one destination but many. Some islands are volcanic and green, others flat and ringed by reef; some are lively with music and rum shacks, others are hushed and luxurious.

## Islands we love for couples

- **St. Lucia.** The Pitons rise straight out of the sea near Soufrière, where resorts like [Jade Mountain](/resorts/jade-mountain/) and [Sugar Beach, A Viceroy Resort](/resorts/sugar-beach-viceroy/) sit among the peaks.
- **Turks and Caicos.** Grace Bay on Providenciales is famous for its calm, clear water and fine sand.
- **Antigua.** The island markets a beach for every day of the year. [Hammock Cove](/resorts/hammock-cove/) is an adults-only all-inclusive where every villa has a plunge pool.
- **Aruba.** Reliable sunshine, trade winds and a location south of the main hurricane belt.
- **Grand Cayman.** Seven Mile Beach and Stingray City's sandbar.
- **Puerto Rico.** Old San Juan's colorful streets and El Yunque National Forest, no passport needed for U.S. citizens.

For all-inclusive islands closer to home, see our pages on [Jamaica](/destinations/jamaica/), the [Bahamas](/destinations/bahamas/) and the [Dominican Republic](/destinations/dominican-republic/).

## When to go

December through April brings dry, sunny weather and peak prices. Late spring can offer a sweet spot. Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30; if you travel then, book flexible rates and consider insurance.
