Florida is the rare destination that can be a quick, affordable weekend or a once-in-a-lifetime splurge. You can wake up in a pastel Art Deco hotel in Miami, drive across the Seven Mile Bridge by lunch and watch the sun melt into the Gulf from Key West that evening, all without a passport.

## Where to go in Florida for couples

**The Florida Keys** are the state's signature romantic escape. The Overseas Highway links the islands from Key Largo to Key West, and each stretch has its own mood: dive shops and mangroves up north, fishing villages in the middle keys, and Key West's lively streets and famous sunsets at the end. See our full guide to [Florida romantic getaways](/guides/best-romantic-getaways-in-florida/).

**The Gulf Coast** trades nightlife for calm water and golden sunsets. Siesta Key, near Sarasota, is known for its fine white quartz sand. St. Pete Beach, Clearwater and Anna Maria Island offer easy-going beach towns within an hour of Tampa's airport.

**The Atlantic coast** is where you'll find Palm Beach's grand resorts, Fort Lauderdale's canals and, further north, Amelia Island's historic town of Fernandina Beach.

**Northwest Florida**, sometimes called the Emerald Coast, is known for green-tinted water and sugar-white dunes along Destin and Scenic Highway 30A.

## When to go

Spring (March to May) and late fall (October to November) are the sweet spots: warm water, lower humidity and fewer afternoon storms. Winter is peak season in South Florida and the Keys, when prices rise and resorts fill up. Summer is hot, humid and stormy, but it can bring lower rates if you are flexible.

## Planning tips

- **Choose one or two bases.** Florida is bigger than it looks; the drive from Miami to Key West alone takes about three and a half to four hours without traffic.
- **Book sunset dinners early** in Key West and on the Gulf Coast, especially on weekends.
- **Plan around the heat.** Schedule outdoor activities for mornings and golden hour.
