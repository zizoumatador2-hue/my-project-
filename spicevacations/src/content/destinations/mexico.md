Mexico makes romance easy. From most U.S. cities it is a short, often nonstop flight, and you land in a world of turquoise water, powdery sand and some of the best adults-only resorts anywhere.

## Choosing your coast

**Cancun and Playa Mujeres.** Cancun's Hotel Zone is a long strip of resorts between the Caribbean and a lagoon. Just north, Playa Mujeres is quieter and home to resorts like [Excellence Playa Mujeres](/resorts/excellence-playa-mujeres/).

**Riviera Maya and Tulum.** South of Cancun, the coast stretches past Playa del Carmen to Tulum. Here you can swim in cenotes (natural sinkholes), snorkel with sea turtles and visit Mayan ruins perched above the sea.

**Los Cabos.** At the southern tip of the Baja California peninsula, Cabo San Lucas and San José del Cabo combine desert mountains with the Pacific. Take a boat to El Arco, the famous rock arch, and look for humpback whales in winter.

**Puerto Vallarta.** A colorful old town, cobblestone streets and a boardwalk malecon lined with sculptures, with jungle hills rising behind the Bay of Banderas.

## When to go

December through April is the dry season and peak time. Hurricane season affects both coasts from roughly late spring or early summer through November, and the Caribbean coast may see sargassum in warmer months.
