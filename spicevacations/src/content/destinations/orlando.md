Orlando is not just for families. Couples come for date nights at the parks, world-class resorts, and a surprising amount of nature just beyond the city.

## Ideas for a couples trip

- **Do the parks your way.** Prioritize thrill rides, nighttime spectaculars and signature restaurants.
- **Eat around the world at EPCOT**, especially during its seasonal food and wine festivals.
- **Escape to Winter Park** for Park Avenue's cafes and the relaxing scenic boat tour.
- **Swim in the springs.** Central Florida's springs stay a constant, refreshing temperature year-round; Blue Spring State Park is known for manatees in winter.
- **Splurge on a resort.** The [Four Seasons Resort Orlando](/resorts/four-seasons-orlando/) has a lazy river, spa and rooftop views of the fireworks.

Orlando pairs well with a few beach nights on either coast. See our [Florida weekend getaways](/guides/florida-weekend-getaways/) guide for ideas.
