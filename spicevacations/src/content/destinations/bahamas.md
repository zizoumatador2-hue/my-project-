The Bahamas feel far away but are remarkably close. From Florida you can be on a Nassau beach in about an hour, and many cities across the East Coast have nonstop service.

## Where to stay

**Nassau and Cable Beach** have the most resorts and flights. Couples-only [Sandals Royal Bahamian](/resorts/sandals-royal-bahamian/) sits on Cable Beach, and the Baha Mar resort complex offers several hotels, a casino and dining.

**The Out Islands** are where you go for quiet. In the Exumas, boat tours visit sandbars, snorkel spots and the swimming pigs. Harbour Island, reached via North Eleuthera, is known for its pink-sand beach and pastel village.

## Getting around

Island-hopping means small flights or ferries, so budget time. Many couples spend a few nights in Nassau and a few in the Out Islands.
