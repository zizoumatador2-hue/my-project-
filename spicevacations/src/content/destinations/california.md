California's romance comes from its landscapes: the fog lifting off Big Sur's cliffs, vineyards glowing in late afternoon light, and palm-lined beaches where the day ends with a Pacific sunset.

## Classic California getaways for couples

**Big Sur and Carmel.** The drive along Highway 1 between Carmel and San Simeon is one of the most scenic in the country. Stay in Carmel-by-the-Sea for storybook charm, or book a cliffside splurge like [Post Ranch Inn](/resorts/post-ranch-inn/) or [Ventana Big Sur](/resorts/ventana-big-sur/), both adults-only.

**Napa and Sonoma.** Napa is polished, with celebrated restaurants and destination wineries; Sonoma feels more rustic and relaxed. Book tastings in advance, pick a designated driver or hire a car service, and spend one afternoon doing nothing at your inn. [Auberge du Soleil](/resorts/auberge-du-soleil/) overlooks Napa Valley from an olive-covered hillside.

**Santa Barbara.** An easy getaway from Los Angeles, with Spanish Colonial architecture, a walkable downtown, wine tasting in the Funk Zone and beaches backed by mountains.

**San Diego and Coronado.** Year-round sunshine, a laid-back beach culture and the grand Hotel del Coronado on the sand.

**Palm Springs.** Mid-century modern design, poolside cocktails and desert spa days, especially lovely from fall through spring.

## When to go

September and October are often the best months on the coast, after summer fog lifts. Spring brings wildflowers and green hills. Winter is quieter and can be a good time for Palm Springs and wine country.
