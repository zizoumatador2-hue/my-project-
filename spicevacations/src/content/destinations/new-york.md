New York City's romance is in its energy: a shared bagel on a bench in Central Park, a Broadway show, a candlelit dinner in the West Village and the skyline lighting up at dusk. It is also the launchpad for some of the best weekend getaways in the country.

## Romantic ideas in the city

- **Walk the Brooklyn Bridge** early in the morning, before the crowds, then get breakfast in DUMBO.
- **Explore Central Park** on foot or by bike; visit Bethesda Terrace and Bow Bridge.
- **See the skyline** from an observation deck or a rooftop bar at sunset.
- **Stroll the High Line**, the elevated park on Manhattan's West Side.
- **Take the ferry.** The Staten Island Ferry is free and passes the Statue of Liberty.

## Escape the city

When you need quiet, New York makes it easy. Take the train up the Hudson to river towns and farm-to-table restaurants, stay in a Victorian castle like [Mohonk Mountain House](/resorts/mohonk-mountain-house/), or head east to the beaches of the Hamptons and Montauk.
