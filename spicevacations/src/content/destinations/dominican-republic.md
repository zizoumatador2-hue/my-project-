The Dominican Republic shares the island of Hispaniola with Haiti and is one of the most visited countries in the Caribbean. For couples, it is all about beaches, value and variety.

## Where to stay

**Punta Cana and Bávaro** sit on the island's eastern tip, with a long coastline of white sand and coconut palms. Resorts range from budget-friendly to indulgent. North of the main strip, Uvero Alto is quieter and home to [Excellence Punta Cana](/resorts/excellence-punta-cana/).

**Cap Cana** is an upscale, gated area just south, with marinas and golf courses. [Sanctuary Cap Cana](/resorts/sanctuary-cap-cana/) is an adults-only resort here.

## Beyond the resort

Take a catamaran to Saona Island, walk the cobblestones of Santo Domingo's Colonial Zone, or visit Samaná Bay in winter to see humpback whales.
