Las Vegas can be as romantic as you make it. Beyond the slot machines are some of the best restaurants and shows in the country, dramatic desert landscapes within an hour's drive, and hotel suites with floor-to-ceiling views of the Strip.

## Romantic things to do in Las Vegas

- **See a show.** Cirque du Soleil productions, residencies and magic shows run most nights. Book ahead for weekends.
- **Watch the Bellagio fountains.** The free fountain shows run regularly in the afternoon and evening; the view from a lakeside restaurant is even better.
- **Go to the desert.** Hike or drive the scenic loop at Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area, or head to Valley of Fire State Park for sunset.
- **Take to the sky.** Helicopter tours fly over the Strip at night or out to the Grand Canyon.
- **Book a spa day.** Many Strip resorts have large spas with thermal pools.

## Where to stay

Choose based on vibe. The [Bellagio](/resorts/bellagio/) delivers classic Vegas glamour and fountain views. The [Waldorf Astoria Las Vegas](/resorts/waldorf-astoria-las-vegas/) has no casino, which makes it a calm retreat just off the Strip.

## When to go

Spring and fall bring comfortable temperatures for desert adventures. Summer is extremely hot, though pool season is in full swing. Midweek stays are often much cheaper than weekends, and big conventions can drive rates up, so be flexible with dates.
