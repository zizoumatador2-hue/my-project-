Hawaii is the ultimate no-passport honeymoon for U.S. travelers. The flights are long from the East Coast, but the reward is a place that feels truly different: black-sand beaches, rainforest waterfalls, sea turtles in the shallows and sunsets that stop conversations.

## Choosing your island

**Maui** balances romance and variety. Wailea and Kaanapali offer polished resorts on golden beaches, the Road to Hana winds past waterfalls and bamboo forests, and Haleakala's summit is famous for sunrise above the clouds. West Maui communities are still rebuilding after the 2023 wildfires, so travel respectfully and support local businesses.

**Kauai** is for couples who want nature first. Take a boat or helicopter tour along the Na Pali Coast, hike the rim of Waimea Canyon and unwind in Poipu on the sunny south shore.

**Hawaii Island** (the Big Island) is big and diverse. Kona's sunny coast has resort beaches and manta ray night snorkels; the east side has Hilo's gardens and access to Hawaii Volcanoes National Park.

**Oahu** is the most convenient, with the most flights. Pair a few nights in Waikiki with time on the North Shore.

## Island hopping

With a week, stick to one island; with ten days or more, two islands work well. Inter-island flights are short, but each move costs you half a day.

## When to go

April to May and September to October usually bring good weather and fewer crowds. Winter is peak season and humpback whale season, roughly December through April. Summer is busy with families.
