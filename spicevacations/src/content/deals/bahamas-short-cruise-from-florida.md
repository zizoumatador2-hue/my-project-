A short cruise is a low-effort way to see the Bahamas. Many three- and four-night sailings leave Florida ports and call at Nassau and a private island owned by the cruise line.

Compare current sailings on the cruise lines' own websites, where fares and cabin availability are set. Not sure a cruise is right for you? Use [Plan My Vacation](/plan-my-vacation/) to compare it with a resort stay, and read our [cruise vacations](/vacation-types/cruise-vacations/) overview.
