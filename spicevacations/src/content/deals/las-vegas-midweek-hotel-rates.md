Las Vegas is a weekend city, and hotel pricing often reflects that. When demand falls between Sunday and Thursday, many resorts lower rates, sometimes significantly.

## Making the most of midweek

Pair a Monday-to-Thursday stay with a show and a desert day trip. Restaurants are easier to book, pools are less crowded, and you might afford a nicer hotel than on a weekend.
