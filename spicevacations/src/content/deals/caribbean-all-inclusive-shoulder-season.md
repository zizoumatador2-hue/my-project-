The Caribbean's peak season runs roughly from mid-December through Easter, when North American travelers escape winter. Once that rush ends, many resorts see demand dip, even though the weather is typically still sunny and dry.

## How to use this window

Start by choosing two or three resorts you would genuinely be happy with, then compare the same room category across several date combinations. Flexible travelers can often find meaningfully better value by shifting a trip by a few days.

## What we don't do

We never publish invented "from" prices or fake countdown timers. Rates change constantly and are set by the resort or booking partner. Use the button on this page to check live availability for your dates.
