Hawaii's busiest periods are summer, the winter holidays and spring break. In between, especially in fall, crowds thin and beaches feel more relaxed.

## How to use this window

Pick your island, then compare several date ranges and lengths of stay. Many resorts offer occasional longer-stay promotions, but terms change often, so always check live offers.
