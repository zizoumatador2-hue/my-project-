Summer is low season on Mexico's Caribbean coast. With fewer winter-escape travelers, adults-only resorts may offer better value on premium room types.

## Trade-offs to weigh

It is hot and humid, afternoon showers are common, and it is hurricane season. Sargassum can also affect some beaches in warmer months. None of these mean you should not go, but they should shape how you book.
