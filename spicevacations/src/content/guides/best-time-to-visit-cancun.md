Cancun is warm all year, so the real question is what you want to trade off: perfect weather and higher prices, or better value with more heat, rain or seaweed. This guide breaks it down month by month, with the two factors most first-timers miss: hurricane season and sargassum seaweed.

## Cancun by season at a glance

| Season | Months | Weather | Crowds and prices |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dry season (peak) | December–April | Sunny, lower humidity, warm days and pleasant evenings | Highest, especially holidays and spring break |
| Shoulder | Late April–May, November | Hot in May, drying out in November | Moderate; often good value |
| Rainy and hurricane season | June–October | Hot and humid, short heavy showers; storm risk highest August–October | Lowest, except July and August school holidays |

## Month by month

| Month | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| January | Dry and sunny, with the coolest evenings of the year. Prices ease after New Year's week. |
| February | One of the most reliable months for weather. Valentine's and Presidents' Day weekends are busy. |
| March | Warm and dry. Spring break brings crowds and higher rates for much of the month. |
| April | Still dry and getting hotter. Easter week is busy; late April is a sweet spot. |
| May | Hot, before the rains start. Sargassum may start to appear. Good value. |
| June | Rainy season begins and hurricane season opens June 1. Humid, with afternoon showers. |
| July | Hot and humid; U.S. summer travel keeps family resorts busy. |
| August | Among the hottest months, and peak hurricane season begins. |
| September | Usually the wettest month and the quietest for crowds, with some of the lowest rates. |
| October | Rain eases late in the month, though storm risk continues. |
| November | Drier and more comfortable as the month goes on. A strong shoulder month outside Thanksgiving week. |
| December | Dry season returns. Christmas to New Year's is the most expensive week of the year. |

## Hurricane season: what it means for your trip

The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30, and activity typically peaks from August through October. Most weeks pass without a storm affecting Cancun, but if one does, it can mean canceled flights or a few days indoors. If you travel in these months:

- Choose flexible or refundable bookings where possible.
- Consider travel insurance that covers weather, bought soon after you book.
- Follow forecasts from the U.S. National Hurricane Center in the week before you leave.

## Sargassum seaweed

Sargassum is a floating seaweed that washes up on many Caribbean beaches. In the Cancun area it tends to show up in spring and is usually heaviest in summer, but some years are much worse than others, and conditions change from week to week. A few practical points:

- Beaches facing the open Caribbean, including much of the Riviera Maya, tend to see more seaweed than sheltered areas such as Isla Mujeres and parts of Playa Mujeres.
- Many resorts rake their beaches each morning and use offshore barriers, which helps but does not solve the problem on heavy days.
- Mexico's federal authorities publish sargassum monitoring updates, and local news and recent traveler photos are a good real-time check.

If a seaweed-free beach matters most to you, the dry season months of December through March are usually the safest bet.

## Best time to visit Cancun for couples

- **For guaranteed sunshine:** January to March, booked early.
- **For value:** late April to early May, or mid-November to mid-December.

## Cancun or Riviera Maya?

The Cancun Hotel Zone is lively, close to the airport and lined with big resorts. Playa Mujeres, just north, is calmer. South of Cancun, the Riviera Maya toward Playa del Carmen and Tulum is more spread out, with smaller resorts like [Secrets Maroma Beach](/resorts/secrets-maroma-beach/) and longer airport transfers.
