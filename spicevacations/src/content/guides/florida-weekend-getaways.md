Florida is made for weekend getaways. With beaches on both coasts, islands linked by bridges and charming historic towns, there's a romantic escape within a few hours of almost anywhere in the state, and a short flight from much of the East Coast.

This guide shares 12 Florida weekend getaways for couples, with easy two-night plans. For longer trips, see [the best romantic getaways in Florida](/guides/best-romantic-getaways-in-florida/), and for broader planning, our complete [romantic getaways](/) guide.

## Island weekends

### 1. Key West

Key West packs a lot into a small island: historic streets, garden restaurants, lively bars and the daily sunset celebration at Mallory Square. Fly directly into Key West to maximize a weekend, or make the Overseas Highway drive part of the adventure. [Casa Marina](/resorts/casa-marina-key-west/) offers a beach resort feel.

**Weekend plan:** Friday sunset at Mallory Square and dinner in Old Town. Saturday bike tour, snorkel trip and a sunset sail. Sunday brunch and a visit to the Hemingway Home before leaving.

### 2. Key Largo and Islamorada

For a quieter Keys weekend closer to Miami, base yourselves in Key Largo or Islamorada. Snorkel at John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park, kayak the mangroves and eat fresh seafood on the water.

## Gulf Coast weekends

### 3. St. Pete Beach

An easy beach weekend with wide sand and great sunsets. [The Don CeSar](/resorts/the-don-cesar/) has been the area's pink landmark since 1928. Pass-a-Grille's historic beach village and downtown St. Petersburg's waterfront museums are nearby.

**Weekend plan:** Friday sunset on the beach. Saturday paddleboarding, pool time and dinner in Pass-a-Grille. Sunday brunch downtown and a stroll along the waterfront.

### 4. Siesta Key

Siesta Key's soft white quartz sand is famous. Stay in or near Siesta Key Village for casual restaurants, and visit Sarasota's museums and gardens.

### 5. Sanibel and Captiva

These barrier islands are about slowing down: shelling at sunrise, biking through wildlife refuges and watching the sunset from Captiva. Check which lodgings and restaurants are open, as the islands have been rebuilding after recent hurricanes.

### 6. Naples

Naples combines an upscale downtown with calm Gulf beaches. Stroll Fifth Avenue South, walk the Naples Pier at sunset and dine outdoors.

## Atlantic coast weekends

### 7. Palm Beach

Palm Beach is polished and glamorous. Shop Worth Avenue, bike the Lake Trail and relax on the beach. [The Breakers](/resorts/the-breakers-palm-beach/) is the classic place to stay.

### 8. Miami Beach

Sunrise on the sand, Art Deco walks, Wynwood's murals and Latin music in Little Havana. See [Miami romantic getaways](/guides/miami-romantic-getaways/) for a full plan.

### 9. Cocoa Beach

A laid-back surf town near the Kennedy Space Center. If you time it right, you may be able to watch a rocket launch from the beach; check the launch schedule.

## Historic and charming weekends

### 10. St. Augustine

Founded in 1565, St. Augustine is the oldest continuously inhabited European-founded city in the United States. Walk St. George Street, visit the Castillo de San Marcos and enjoy the Nights of Lights during the winter holiday season.

### 11. Amelia Island

Victorian Fernandina Beach, 13 miles of beaches and horseback riding on the sand. It's quiet, charming and romantic.

## Central Florida

### 12. Winter Park

Just north of Orlando, Winter Park has brick streets, boutiques, cafés and a scenic boat tour through its chain of lakes. Pair it with a day at the parks or a spring swim. The [Four Seasons Resort Orlando](/resorts/four-seasons-orlando/) is a luxurious base.

## When to go

- **March to May:** warm days, cooler evenings and lower humidity.
- **October to November:** pleasant weather and fewer crowds.
- **December to April:** peak season in South Florida and the Keys.
- **June to September:** hot, humid and stormy; hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30.

## Tips for a Florida weekend getaway

- **Fly in Friday morning** or leave early to maximize time.
- **Book sunset dinners ahead**, especially on the Gulf Coast.
- **Watch the weather.** Summer storms usually pass quickly in the afternoon.
- **Choose one base** to avoid wasting time driving.
- **Use reef-safe sunscreen**, especially in the Keys.

## What to pack

Light clothing, two swimsuits each, sandals, a light rain jacket, sunscreen, a hat and one dressier outfit for dinner. Bug spray helps at sunset in the Keys and on Gulf islands.

## Romantic Florida experiences worth planning

- **A sunset sail in Key West** or on the Gulf Coast.
- **Kayaking through mangroves** in the Keys or on Sanibel.
- **A rocket launch** viewed from Cocoa Beach, if the schedule aligns.
- **A springs swim** in Central Florida's crystal-clear, spring-fed waters.
- **A carriage ride** through historic St. Augustine.
- **Horseback riding on the beach** on Amelia Island.
- **A couples massage** at a Palm Beach or Naples spa.

## Florida weekends by season

**Winter:** South Florida and the Keys are warm and sunny, perfect for beach weekends, but book early. North Florida and St. Augustine are cooler, ideal for exploring on foot.

**Spring:** The sweet spot almost everywhere. Gulf Coast water warms up, and humidity stays low. Avoid spring-break weeks in beach towns if you want quiet.

**Summer:** Plan beach time for mornings and late afternoons, and expect short afternoon storms. Springs are especially refreshing.

**Fall:** Crowds thin after Labor Day. October and November bring pleasant weather, though you should watch hurricane forecasts until the end of November.

## Making a short Florida trip feel longer

Fly into the airport closest to your destination, even if it costs a little more; the time you save is worth it on a weekend. Choose a hotel within walking distance of the beach and restaurants so you don't need to drive. And plan your first evening around sunset: nothing kicks off a Florida weekend like watching the sky turn pink over the water.

## A sample weekend: Sanibel and Captiva

**Friday:** Fly into Fort Myers, cross the causeway and check into a beach cottage or small resort. Walk the beach at sunset. **Saturday:** Rise early to hunt for shells, rent bikes to explore the J.N. "Ding" Darling National Wildlife Refuge and drive up to Captiva for sunset and dinner. **Sunday:** Paddle a kayak through the mangroves, have a relaxed brunch and head back to the airport.

## A sample weekend: St. Augustine and Amelia Island

**Friday:** Arrive in St. Augustine, walk St. George Street and have dinner in the historic district. **Saturday:** Tour the Castillo de San Marcos, climb the St. Augustine Lighthouse and drive north to Amelia Island for sunset on the beach. **Sunday:** Browse Fernandina Beach's shops and cafés before heading home.

## Where to stay for a romantic Florida weekend

For a beach weekend, choose a hotel on or across the street from the sand so you can walk to sunrise and sunset. In historic towns like St. Augustine and Fernandina Beach, bed-and-breakfasts and boutique inns within walking distance of restaurants feel more romantic than chain hotels. For a splurge, Florida's grand historic resorts, like The Breakers and The Don CeSar, add a sense of occasion.

## Getting around

In Key West, Palm Beach, St. Augustine and Winter Park, you can explore on foot or by bike, so a rental car is optional. For Gulf Coast islands, Sanibel and Amelia Island, a car makes it easier to reach beaches and restaurants. Build in extra time on Friday afternoons, when traffic around Florida's big cities can be heavy.
