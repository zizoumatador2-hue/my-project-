A weekend getaway is the most underrated romantic trip. It doesn't need vacation days, it costs a fraction of a big trip and it can reset a relationship that's been running on autopilot. The trick is choosing a place that's close enough to reach easily and different enough to feel like an escape.

This guide shares 20 weekend getaways for couples across the U.S., organized by region, plus a simple plan for making two nights feel like a real vacation. For longer trips, see our complete [romantic getaways](/) guide.

## The three-hour rule

The most important decision for a weekend trip is distance. Keep one-way travel to about three hours door to door. That can mean a two-and-a-half-hour drive, a short train ride or a one-hour flight plus an easy airport. If you leave Friday after work and come home Sunday evening, every extra hour of travel comes straight out of your time together.

## Weekend getaways in the Northeast

### 1. The Hudson Valley, New York

River towns, farm-to-table restaurants, orchards and hiking, a short train ride or drive from New York City. [Mohonk Mountain House](/resorts/mohonk-mountain-house/), a Victorian castle on a mountain lake, is a New York classic. See our [best weekend getaways from New York](/guides/best-weekend-getaways-from-new-york/) for more.

### 2. Newport, Rhode Island

Walk the Cliff Walk past Gilded Age mansions, sail on Narragansett Bay and eat lobster rolls by the harbor.

### 3. Cape May, New Jersey

Victorian bed-and-breakfasts, a walkable beach town and beautiful sunsets at Sunset Beach. Lovely in late spring and early fall.

### 4. The Berkshires, Massachusetts

Tanglewood concerts in summer, foliage drives in fall and cozy inns year-round.

### 5. Stowe, Vermont

A quintessential New England town with mountain views, covered bridges and fireside dinners, especially romantic in fall and winter.

## Weekend getaways in the Southeast

### 6. Charleston, South Carolina

Rainbow Row, gas-lit alleys, rooftop bars and some of the South's best restaurants.

### 7. Savannah, Georgia

Oak-shaded squares, Spanish moss and Forsyth Park's fountain. Compact, walkable and full of atmosphere.

### 8. St. Augustine, Florida

The oldest continuously inhabited European-founded city in the U.S., with Spanish colonial streets, a historic fort and nearby beaches.

### 9. St. Pete Beach, Florida

Gulf sunsets, a wide sandy beach and [The Don CeSar](/resorts/the-don-cesar/), the "Pink Palace" that has stood on the beach since 1928. See [Florida weekend getaways](/guides/florida-weekend-getaways/).

### 10. The Florida Keys

A long weekend is just enough for Islamorada's calm water or a sunset celebration in Key West. Fly into Key West to skip the drive, or make the Overseas Highway part of the adventure.

### 11. Asheville, North Carolina

Blue Ridge Mountain views, an artsy downtown, craft breweries and the grand Biltmore Estate.

## Weekend getaways in the Midwest

### 12. Door County, Wisconsin

Lighthouses, cherry orchards and small harbor towns along Lake Michigan. Summer and early fall are ideal.

### 13. Chicago, Illinois

Architecture boat tours on the Chicago River, lakefront walks, deep-dish pizza and jazz clubs.

### 14. Lake Geneva, Wisconsin

Historic lakefront estates, a scenic shore path and a relaxed small-town feel.

## Weekend getaways in the West and Southwest

### 15. Napa and Sonoma, California

Wine tastings, long lunches and country inns. [Auberge du Soleil](/resorts/auberge-du-soleil/) overlooks the valley. Book tastings ahead and arrange a driver.

### 16. Santa Barbara, California

Spanish Colonial architecture, beaches, mountains and a walkable wine district.

### 17. Palm Springs, California

Desert pools, mid-century design and spa days. Best from October to April.

### 18. Sedona, Arizona

Red-rock hikes, spa treatments and dazzling night skies.

### 19. Las Vegas, Nevada

Shows, dinners and desert day trips. Hotels like the [Bellagio](/resorts/bellagio/) offer fountain-view rooms. Midweek stays are often better value. See our [Las Vegas couples vacation](/guides/las-vegas-couples-vacation/) guide.

### 20. Lake Tahoe, California and Nevada

Clear blue water in summer, snow and fireplaces in winter, and scenic drives year-round.

## Weekend getaways by season

**Spring.** This is the season for gardens and wildflowers. Charleston and Savannah are in bloom, California's wine country is green, and the Florida Keys are warm without summer's humidity. Book around spring-break weeks if you want quiet beaches.

**Summer.** Head for water or elevation. Lake towns, the New England coast and mountain resorts stay comfortable when cities swelter. Book early, because summer weekends fill quickly in popular getaway spots.

**Fall.** Fall is the best season for weekend getaways in much of the country. Foliage peaks in New England and the Hudson Valley from late September into October, wine country celebrates harvest, and desert destinations like Sedona and Palm Springs become pleasant again.

**Winter.** Choose between warmth and coziness. South Florida and the Keys are at their best, while snowy mountain inns with fireplaces offer a different kind of romance. Cities are festive in December and often quieter, with better hotel rates, in January.

## Three ready-made weekend plans

### Hudson Valley in the fall

**Friday:** Take the train or drive north from New York City, check into a country inn and have dinner at a farm-to-table restaurant. **Saturday:** Hike a ridge trail for foliage views, go apple picking in the afternoon and share a bottle of local cider at sunset. **Sunday:** Browse antique shops in a river town, have a long brunch and head home.

### Napa Valley in spring

**Friday:** Arrive in the afternoon and settle into your inn. Have a relaxed dinner in town. **Saturday:** Book two tastings with a long lunch in between, then watch the sunset from your terrace. **Sunday:** Take a morning bike ride through the vineyards before heading home.

### The Gulf Coast in late fall

**Friday:** Check into a beachfront hotel and catch the sunset from the sand. **Saturday:** Rent kayaks or paddleboards in the morning, nap by the pool, and book a sunset cruise. **Sunday:** Walk the beach at sunrise looking for shells, have breakfast by the water and drive home.

## What to pack for a weekend away

Keep it light: one carry-on each is plenty. Bring comfortable walking shoes, one dressier outfit for your special dinner, layers for cool evenings, a swimsuit if there's a pool or hot tub, and a small speaker for music in your room. A paper book or a deck of cards encourages you to put your phones down.

## How to make a weekend feel like a vacation

**Leave early or come back late.** Taking Friday afternoon off or returning Monday morning turns two nights into a much more relaxing trip.

**Stay in one place.** Packing and unpacking twice in 48 hours kills the mood.

**Book the highlight first.** Reserve your special dinner or spa appointment when you book your room.

**Unplug.** Agree on phone-free meals, and leave work email alone.

**Upgrade the room, not the itinerary.** On a short trip, a balcony, fireplace or view gets used constantly and makes the weekend feel special.

## Surprise weekend ideas

A surprise weekend is one of the most romantic gifts you can give. To pull it off, secretly clear your partner's calendar (enlist a coworker or friend if needed), pack a bag for them with a note inside, and tell them only what to wear. Choose a destination within a short drive so the reveal comes quickly. Plan the first evening in detail, like a sunset spot and a favorite type of food, and keep the rest of the weekend flexible so it feels relaxed rather than choreographed. Keep the budget in line with what you'd normally spend together, so the surprise is a delight rather than a worry.

## Cheap weekend getaways for couples

A romantic weekend doesn't have to be expensive. Try these ideas:

- **Drive instead of fly.** Choose somewhere within a two- or three-hour drive.
- **Go off-season.** Beach towns in spring and fall, and mountain towns between ski and summer seasons, often have better rates.
- **Travel Sunday to Tuesday.** Many hotels charge less on weeknights.
- **Stay in a state park cabin or small inn** instead of a big resort.
- **Picnic once.** A sunset picnic with local cheese and a bottle of wine is more romantic than many restaurant meals.

## Make it a tradition

Many couples turn the weekend getaway into an annual ritual: the same inn every anniversary, a new city every spring or a different state park every fall. Traditions give you something to look forward to all year, and repeating a favorite place lets you notice how you've both changed since the last visit.
