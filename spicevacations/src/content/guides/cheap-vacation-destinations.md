Traveling as a couple doesn't have to drain your savings. The best cheap vacation destinations combine affordable lodging, lots of flight options and plenty of free or low-cost things to do. Choose the right place and time, and you can have a memorable trip for far less than you'd expect.

This guide covers 18 affordable destinations in the U.S., Mexico and the Caribbean, with why each is good value and how to keep costs down. For more budget advice, see our complete [romantic getaways](/) guide.

## What makes a destination cheap?

The most affordable destinations usually share a few traits:

- **Lots of competition:** many hotels and flights keep prices in check.
- **Free attractions:** beaches, parks, hiking trails and walkable neighborhoods.
- **Affordable food:** local restaurants, food trucks and markets.
- **Easy transportation:** walkable towns or cheap public transit.
- **Clear low and shoulder seasons** when prices ease.

## Cheap vacation destinations in the U.S.

### 1. Florida's Gulf Coast

St. Pete Beach, Clearwater, Fort Myers Beach and Anna Maria Island have beautiful beaches and sunsets, often at lower prices than South Florida. Spring and late fall are ideal. Splurge on a sunset drink at [The Don CeSar](/resorts/the-don-cesar/).

### 2. Las Vegas (midweek)

With an enormous supply of hotel rooms, Las Vegas can be very affordable Sunday to Thursday. Free attractions include the Bellagio fountains and Conservatory. See [Las Vegas couples vacation](/guides/las-vegas-couples-vacation/).

### 3. Orlando (off-peak)

Orlando has a huge number of hotels, keeping many rates competitive outside school holidays. Beyond the parks, enjoy springs, lakes and Winter Park.

### 4. Savannah, Georgia

Savannah's best experiences are free: walking its squares, Forsyth Park and the riverfront. Southern food is affordable and delicious.

### 5. New Orleans, Louisiana

Free live music on Frenchmen Street, historic neighborhoods and affordable po'boys and beignets. Avoid major festival weekends for better rates.

### 6. The Great Smoky Mountains

Great Smoky Mountains National Park has no entrance fee (a parking tag is required for longer stops). Cabins in nearby towns can be good value outside peak fall weekends.

### 7. Gulf Shores and Orange Beach, Alabama

Sugar-white beaches and warm Gulf water, often at lower prices than Florida.

### 8. San Antonio, Texas

The River Walk, historic missions (a UNESCO World Heritage Site) and Tex-Mex food make San Antonio a fun, affordable city break.

### 9. Asheville, North Carolina

Blue Ridge Mountain hikes, a creative downtown and craft breweries. Visit outside peak foliage weekends for lower prices.

### 10. The Outer Banks, North Carolina (shoulder season)

Wild beaches, lighthouses and dunes. Spring and fall bring lower rates than summer.

### 11. Chicago (winter or midweek)

Chicago's architecture, lakefront and museums are world-class. January and February hotel rates are often lower.

### 12. Albuquerque and Santa Fe, New Mexico

Adobe architecture, art, green chile and desert landscapes. Albuquerque is often more affordable than Santa Fe as a base.

## Cheap international destinations

### 13. Cancun and the Riviera Maya (shoulder season)

Cancun is one of the most connected airports for U.S. travelers, with a huge range of lodging from budget hotels to all-inclusive resorts. Late April through early June can offer good value.

### 14. Punta Cana, Dominican Republic

Many all-inclusive resorts compete for guests, often keeping prices reasonable. [Excellence Punta Cana](/resorts/excellence-punta-cana/) is an adults-only choice.

### 15. Mexico City

Mexico City offers world-class museums, markets and food at prices that feel accessible, plus beautiful neighborhoods like Roma and Coyoacán.

### 16. Puerto Vallarta, Mexico

A walkable old town, a seaside malecon and affordable seafood. Summer is low season.

### 17. Puerto Rico

No passport needed for U.S. citizens, lots of flights and free experiences like Old San Juan's streets and beaches.

### 18. Short Bahamas cruises

Short cruises from Florida bundle lodging, many meals and entertainment into one fare. Watch for extras.

## How to keep any trip cheap

1. **Travel in shoulder season.** It's the biggest money saver.
2. **Fly midweek** and compare nearby airports.
3. **Stay somewhere walkable** to save on transport.
4. **Eat big at lunch**, when many restaurants are cheaper.
5. **Shop at local markets** for picnics.
6. **Use free activities:** beaches, parks, museums with free days, festivals.
7. **Consider all-inclusive** when you'll eat and drink at the resort.
8. **Compare total prices** including fees.

## Affordable doesn't mean less romantic

Some of the most romantic trips are simple: a sunset walk on a Gulf beach, live jazz on a New Orleans street, or a picnic in a Savannah square. What matters is time together and a change of scenery. Read [affordable romantic getaways](/guides/affordable-romantic-getaways/) for more ideas.

## Sample budget trip: Savannah long weekend

**Friday:** Drive or fly in and check into a well-located inn. Walk the squares at dusk and have dinner at a casual Southern restaurant. **Saturday:** Stroll Forsyth Park, tour a historic home, picnic in a square and watch the ships pass on River Street. **Sunday:** Drive to nearby Tybee Island for a beach morning before heading home.

## Cheap destinations by season

**Winter:** Big cities like Chicago, New York and Washington, D.C., often have lower hotel rates in January and February. Mexico City is pleasant and affordable in winter.

**Spring:** Shoulder season begins in the Caribbean and Mexico after Easter. The Great Smoky Mountains bloom with wildflowers.

**Summer:** Las Vegas and Palm Springs are hot but often cheaper. Summer is low season in Cancun and Punta Cana, with hurricane-season trade-offs.

**Fall:** Beach towns quiet down after Labor Day, and the Gulf Coast can be lovely in October, though you should watch hurricane forecasts.

## Where to splurge on a cheap trip

Even on a budget, one well-chosen splurge makes a trip feel special. On the Gulf Coast, book a sunset sail. In New Orleans, have dinner at a classic Creole restaurant. In Las Vegas, see a show. In Mexico City, book a food tour. In Punta Cana, upgrade to a room with a view. Choose the one experience you'll remember most and budget for it first.

## Cheap travel mistakes to avoid

1. **Choosing the cheapest hotel far from everything.** You'll spend more on transport and lose time.
2. **Ignoring fees.** Resort fees, baggage fees and parking can erase savings.
3. **Traveling in peak holidays.** Prices can double around major holidays.
4. **Skipping travel insurance on nonrefundable international trips** during hurricane season.
5. **Overpacking your itinerary** with paid activities; mix in free experiences.

## How to decide between two cheap destinations

When two destinations look similar in price, compare what's free to do there, how walkable they are and how much you'd spend on food. A destination with beaches, parks and street life you can enjoy without spending money usually delivers more value than one where every activity costs extra. Consider travel time too; a slightly more expensive destination with a nonstop flight may be better than a cheaper one with a long connection.

## Road trip ideas on a budget

A road trip is one of the cheapest ways for a couple to travel, since two people share the cost of gas and there are no airfares or baggage fees. Consider the Blue Ridge Parkway in fall, the Florida Keys' Overseas Highway, California's Highway 1, the Great River Road along the Mississippi or a loop through New Mexico's high desert. Pack a cooler for picnic lunches, stay in small towns rather than big resort areas, and build your days around free scenic stops.

## Free things couples love in cheap destinations

Walking tours you can do yourself with a downloaded map, sunset from a public beach or pier, local farmers markets, free museum days, live music in parks and plazas, state park hikes and scenic drives. Build your itinerary around these, and add one or two paid highlights.

## Final tip

Start a shared travel fund and set price alerts for two or three dream destinations. When a good price appears, you'll be ready to book.
