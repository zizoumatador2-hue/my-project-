Your honeymoon is often the first big trip you take as a married couple, and it comes right after months of wedding planning. The best honeymoon destinations make things easy: beautiful settings, excellent service and plenty of time to do absolutely nothing together.

This guide covers 14 honeymoon destinations for U.S. couples, from no-passport paradises to once-in-a-lifetime journeys. For general planning advice, including budgets and timing, see our complete [romantic getaways](/) guide.

## How to choose your honeymoon destination

Before falling in love with photos, answer these questions together:

1. **How much time do you have?** A long weekend, a week or two weeks changes everything.
2. **What's your budget?** Include flights, lodging, food and experiences.
3. **When are you traveling?** Weather and hurricane season matter.
4. **What's the vibe?** Beach and relaxation, adventure, culture or a mix?
5. **Do you both have valid passports?** Name changes after the wedding can complicate international travel. Book tickets in the name on your current passport.

## No-passport honeymoon destinations

### Maui, Hawaii

Maui is the classic American honeymoon. Wailea's resort beaches are ideal for swimming, the Road to Hana winds past waterfalls, and Haleakala's summit offers sunrise above the clouds (reservations required). The adults-only [Hotel Wailea](/resorts/hotel-wailea/) is an intimate, all-suite retreat on the hillside. Read our [Hawaii couples vacation](/guides/hawaii-couples-vacation/) guide.

### Kauai, Hawaii

Kauai is quieter and wilder, with the dramatic Na Pali Coast, Waimea Canyon and sunny Poipu beaches. It's perfect for couples who'd rather hike and explore than shop.

### The Florida Keys

For a shorter or more affordable honeymoon, the Keys deliver island vibes and spectacular sunsets. For total seclusion, [Little Palm Island](/resorts/little-palm-island/) is an adults-only private island reached by boat or seaplane. See [the best romantic getaways in Florida](/guides/best-romantic-getaways-in-florida/).

### Big Sur and Napa Valley

A California road trip combines clifftop drives, redwood forests and wine country. Splurge on a stay at Post Ranch Inn or Ventana Big Sur, then slow down in Napa's vineyards.

### Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands

Caribbean beaches without a passport for U.S. citizens. Explore Old San Juan's colorful streets, El Yunque's rainforest and quiet island beaches.

## Caribbean honeymoon destinations

### St. Lucia

St. Lucia is one of the most romantic places on Earth. The twin Pitons rise from the sea near Soufrière, where [Jade Mountain](/resorts/jade-mountain/) offers suites with no fourth wall and many private infinity pools. Add rainforest hikes, volcanic mud baths and catamaran sails.

### Antigua

Antigua is known for its many beaches. [Hammock Cove](/resorts/hammock-cove/), an adults-only all-inclusive, gives every villa its own plunge pool.

### Jamaica

Jamaica's couples-only resorts make it an easy honeymoon. Negril's sunsets and Seven Mile Beach are highlights.

### Turks and Caicos

Grace Bay's calm turquoise water is ideal for honeymooners who simply want the perfect beach.

Read more in [Caribbean vacations for couples](/guides/caribbean-vacations-for-couples/).

## Mexico honeymoon destinations

### The Riviera Maya and Cancun

Mexico's Caribbean coast is the easiest honeymoon for many Americans: short flights, adults-only all-inclusives with swim-up suites, and adventures like cenotes and Mayan ruins. See [the best adults-only resorts in Mexico](/guides/best-adults-only-resorts-in-mexico/).

### Los Cabos

Los Cabos pairs desert mountains with the Pacific, upscale resorts and excellent dining. Winter brings humpback whales.

## Once-in-a-lifetime honeymoon destinations

### Bora Bora, French Polynesia

Bora Bora's lagoon, ringed by a coral reef and overlooked by Mount Otemanu, is the classic overwater-bungalow honeymoon. Flights from the U.S. typically route through Tahiti.

### The Maldives

A chain of coral atolls in the Indian Ocean known for overwater villas, clear water and excellent diving. It's a long journey from the U.S., so plan for at least ten nights.

### Santorini, Greece

Whitewashed villages, blue-domed churches and famous caldera sunsets. Pair it with Athens or another island.

### The Amalfi Coast, Italy

Cliffside villages, lemon groves and long lunches overlooking the Mediterranean. Combine with Rome or Florence for a longer trip.

## Affordable honeymoon destinations

A beautiful honeymoon doesn't have to be expensive. Consider:

- **Punta Cana, Dominican Republic:** all-inclusive value with long beaches.
- **Cancun in shoulder season:** late April through early June often offers better rates.
- **Florida's Gulf Coast:** sunsets and calm water without international flights.
- **Puerto Rico:** Caribbean flavor with no passport required for U.S. citizens.
- **A U.S. road trip:** Napa, Big Sur or New England in fall.

## Combining two destinations

Many couples want both adventure and relaxation, and a two-stop honeymoon can deliver both. Good combinations include Maui and Kauai; Miami and the Florida Keys; a city stay in Mexico City followed by the Riviera Maya; St. Lucia's hillside resorts plus a beach resort; or Athens and Santorini. Keep transfers simple: one flight or a short drive between stops, and at least three nights in each place.

## Honeymoon destinations by season

- **Winter (December–February):** the Caribbean, Mexico and the Florida Keys are at their best; Hawaii offers whale season.
- **Spring (March–May):** Hawaii's shoulder season, St. Lucia and Antigua before summer, California's green hills.
- **Summer (June–August):** Bora Bora's dry season, the Mediterranean (busy and hot in July and August), and adults-only resorts in Mexico with flexible bookings.
- **Fall (September–November):** Hawaii, Napa's harvest season, Santorini in September and early October.

## Honeymoons on different budgets

**A modest budget** works well with drive-to or short-flight destinations, shoulder-season travel and a single special splurge, like a couples massage or a sunset sail.

**A mid-range budget** opens up adults-only all-inclusives in Mexico and the Dominican Republic, or a week in Hawaii with a well-located hotel.

**A generous budget** makes room for St. Lucia's hillside hideaways, private plunge pools, overwater villas in the South Pacific and multi-stop European trips.

## Honeymoon planning timeline

- **6–8 months before:** choose a destination and book flights and resort.
- **3–4 months before:** book special experiences, like helicopter tours or private dinners.
- **1–2 months before:** confirm transfers, check passports and entry rules, and pack.
- **A week before:** tell your resort you're on your honeymoon; some offer small touches, though never guaranteed.

## Honeymoon tips

- **Rest first.** Build in a lazy first day after the wedding.
- **Don't overschedule.** One activity a day is plenty.
- **Check honeymoon perks.** Some resorts offer them with proof of marriage within a set time. Confirm current offers directly.
- **Consider a split stay.** Combine an adventurous base with a relaxing one.
- **Protect your trip.** Consider travel insurance, especially during hurricane season.

## Mini-moons and delayed honeymoons

Not everyone can take two weeks off right after the wedding. A "mini-moon" of three or four nights somewhere close, like the Florida Keys, Napa or a Hudson Valley inn, lets you celebrate immediately and save the big trip for later. Delayed honeymoons are increasingly popular too: waiting a few months can mean better weather, lower prices and more time to plan, and it gives you something wonderful to look forward to after the wedding rush.

## Registry tip

Many couples add honeymoon experiences to their wedding registry: a sunset sail, a couples massage or a special dinner. Guests love contributing to a memory, and it helps make the trip more affordable. Send a postcard or a photo from the experience afterward as a thank-you; it means a lot to the people who gave it, and it becomes part of the story of your first trip as a married couple.
