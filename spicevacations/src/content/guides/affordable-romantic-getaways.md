Romance has nothing to do with price. Some of the most memorable trips couples take are simple: a cabin by a lake, a beach town in the off-season or a city weekend built around long walks and one great dinner. What matters is time together, a change of scenery and a few moments that feel special.

This guide covers 15 affordable romantic getaways and the strategies that make them affordable. For the complete picture on planning, see our main [romantic getaways](/) guide.

## How to think about a budget getaway

Before choosing a destination, understand where the money goes. Most trips have four costs: transportation, lodging, food and experiences. Budget trips work by reducing the biggest one, usually lodging or flights, while protecting a small splurge that makes the trip feel special.

**The formula we like:** save on the big things, splurge on one small thing. Stay in a simple, clean place with a great location, drive instead of fly when possible, and spend on one dinner, one sunset sail or one couples massage.

## Affordable romantic getaways in the U.S.

### 1. Florida's Gulf Coast in shoulder season

St. Pete Beach, Clearwater, Anna Maria Island and Fort Myers Beach offer beautiful sunsets and calm water. Outside peak winter weeks, lodging often costs less than on Florida's Atlantic coast. Treat yourselves to sunset drinks at [The Don CeSar](/resorts/the-don-cesar/) even if you stay elsewhere.

### 2. Midweek Las Vegas

Las Vegas hotels commonly price weekends higher than weeknights. A Sunday-to-Wednesday trip can let you afford a nicer hotel or a show. Free experiences include the Bellagio fountains, the Conservatory & Botanical Gardens and people-watching on the Strip. Our [Las Vegas couples vacation](/guides/las-vegas-couples-vacation/) guide has more.

### 3. Savannah, Georgia

Savannah's greatest pleasures are free: walking its squares, exploring Forsyth Park and admiring historic homes. Food ranges from casual Southern cooking to special-occasion restaurants.

### 4. Charleston, South Carolina

Walk the Battery, Rainbow Row and the historic district's alleys. Visit in late fall or winter for lower rates.

### 5. St. Augustine, Florida

History, walkable streets and nearby beaches make St. Augustine a great-value getaway, especially outside holiday weekends.

### 6. State park cabins and lodges

Many state parks across the country rent cabins or have lodges. They're often among the most affordable romantic getaways available, with hiking, lakes and starry skies included. Book early for peak seasons.

### 7. Small-town inns near home

Search within two hours of where you live. A historic inn in a small town can feel like a world away, and saving on travel lets you splurge on a nicer room.

### 8. Orlando for grown-ups, off-peak

Orlando has a huge supply of hotels, which keeps many prices competitive outside school holidays. Pair a park day with free or low-cost experiences like Winter Park's shops and lakes, and springs in state parks.

### 9. The Texas Hill Country

Wineries, swimming holes and small towns like Fredericksburg offer charm without big-city prices.

### 10. The Great Smoky Mountains

Great Smoky Mountains National Park has no entrance fee, and cabins in the surrounding towns can be good value outside peak fall-color weekends. Note that a parking tag is required for vehicles parked in the park for more than 15 minutes.

## Affordable international getaways

### 11. Shoulder-season Punta Cana

The Dominican Republic is known for all-inclusive value. Traveling after Easter and before summer holidays can improve it further. [Excellence Punta Cana](/resorts/excellence-punta-cana/) is an adults-only option on the quieter Uvero Alto coast.

### 12. Cancun in late spring

Cancun has a vast range of resorts and plenty of flights, which helps keep prices competitive. Late April through early June is often good value before hurricane season. See [all-inclusive vacation deals](/guides/all-inclusive-vacation-deals/).

### 13. Los Cabos in summer

Summer is hot in Los Cabos, but it can bring lower rates at resorts like [Breathless Cabo San Lucas](/resorts/breathless-cabo-san-lucas/). Weigh the trade-offs of heat and hurricane season.

### 14. Puerto Rico

No passport needed for U.S. citizens, lots of flight options and a mix of free experiences, from Old San Juan's streets to beaches and rainforest trails.

### 15. A short Bahamas cruise from Florida

Short cruises bundle lodging, many meals and entertainment into one fare, which can make them good value. Watch for extras like drink packages, gratuities and excursions.

## 12 ways to save on a romantic getaway

1. **Be flexible by a few days.** Shifting your dates is the single biggest money saver.
2. **Travel midweek.** Sunday to Thursday is often cheaper in weekend destinations.
3. **Go in shoulder season.** The weeks just before or after peak season often combine good weather and better rates.
4. **Drive when you can.** Two people driving often cost less than two plane tickets.
5. **Compare total price.** Include resort fees, parking and taxes.
6. **Choose location over luxury.** A walkable hotel saves on taxis and time.
7. **Eat your big meal at lunch.** Many restaurants offer lunch menus that cost less than dinner.
8. **Picnic at sunset.** Local bread, cheese and a view beat an expensive restaurant.
9. **Use free experiences.** Beaches, parks, hikes, festivals and scenic drives.
10. **Consider all-inclusive when you'll use it.** Predictable costs help you stay on budget.
11. **Use points and perks.** Loyalty programs and credit card benefits can cover a night or an upgrade.
12. **Book refundable when possible.** If prices drop, you may be able to rebook.

## Free and low-cost romantic activities

The best moments of a trip often cost nothing. Wherever you go, look for:

- **Sunrise or sunset walks** on a beach, pier or scenic overlook.
- **State and national parks.** Many have free or low entrance fees and offer hiking, swimming and wildlife viewing.
- **Free museum days.** Many city museums offer free admission on certain days or evenings.
- **Farmers markets.** Pick up picnic supplies and taste local specialties.
- **Scenic drives.** Coastal highways, mountain parkways and country roads cost only gas.
- **Stargazing.** Get away from city lights with a blanket and a thermos of something warm.
- **Local festivals.** Music, food and holiday festivals often have free entry.

## Affordable romantic getaways by season

**Winter:** Big-city hotels are often better value in January and February, after the holiday rush. It's also a great time for cozy cabins in the mountains if you don't ski.

**Spring:** The Caribbean and Mexico often become better value after Easter, while weather is still dry.

**Summer:** Look for deals in destinations that are hot in summer, like Las Vegas and Palm Springs, as long as you're happy to spend afternoons in the pool.

**Fall:** Beach towns quiet down after Labor Day, often with warm water and lower rates, though you should keep an eye on hurricane forecasts in the Southeast.

## How to tell a real deal from a fake one

Be wary of countdown timers, "only one room left" warnings you can't verify and "from" prices that apply to dates nobody wants. A real deal is a lower total price, including all fees, for the dates and room you actually want. Compare the same room category across several dates and sites, read the cancellation terms, and check whether taxes and resort fees are included before you decide.

## Where to splurge on a budget trip

The magic of an affordable getaway comes from one well-chosen splurge. Ideas:

- A room with a balcony or ocean view
- A sunset sail or catamaran cruise
- A couples massage
- A tasting menu or chef's table
- A hot air balloon ride in wine country or the desert

## Affordable doesn't mean less romantic

Some of the most romantic travel moments are the simplest: sharing a pastry on a park bench, watching the sun set from a quiet beach or talking late into the night on a cabin porch. Expensive trips can be wonderful, but they can also bring pressure to make every moment perfect. A budget getaway frees you to be spontaneous, and the planning you do together, hunting for the best-value inn or the perfect picnic spot, becomes part of the fun. Many couples say their favorite trips were the ones where they had the least money and the most time.

## Sample budget-friendly weekend

**Friday:** Drive two hours to a small beach town. Check into a simple, well-reviewed inn near the water. Walk to a casual seafood dinner.

**Saturday:** Beach morning with coffee and pastries. Rent bikes. Picnic lunch. Sunset sail (your splurge). Late dinner at a local favorite.

**Sunday:** Slow breakfast, one last walk on the beach, and drive home in the afternoon.

## A final tip

Start a small "getaway fund" and set aside a little each month. When a good deal appears for a place you've been dreaming about, you'll be ready to book without stress.
