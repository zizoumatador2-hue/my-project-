Valentine's Day 2027 has a useful twist: February 14 falls on a Sunday, and Monday, February 15 is Presidents' Day. For many couples that turns Valentine's Day into a three-day weekend, which is long enough for a short flight somewhere warm, or a slow drive to a cabin without rushing home.

The flip side is demand. A holiday weekend that overlaps with Valentine's Day is one of the busiest stretches of February for hotels, restaurants and flights, so the best plans are made early.

## Pick the kind of trip first

| If you want… | Go for | Good examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm weather and a beach | A short flight south | Florida Keys, Mexico's Caribbean coast, the Bahamas |
| A bigger trip | Four to six nights | Hawaii, St. Lucia, Aruba |
| Snow and a fireplace | A drive-to cabin or inn | The Hudson Valley, New England, the Smoky Mountains |
| Food and wine | A quiet wine country weekend | Napa or Sonoma, the Texas Hill Country |
| Shows and restaurants | A city break | New York, Las Vegas, New Orleans |

## Warm-weather Valentine's getaways

February is dry season across much of the Caribbean and Mexico, which makes it one of the most reliable months for sunshine.

- **The Florida Keys.** No passport needed, mild February weather and sunsets over the water. Key West is lively; Islamorada and the Lower Keys are quieter. See [Casa Marina Key West](/resorts/casa-marina-key-west/) and our [romantic getaways in Florida](/guides/best-romantic-getaways-in-florida/).
- **Mexico's Caribbean coast.** Cancun, Playa del Carmen and Tulum have nonstop flights from many U.S. cities. An adults-only all-inclusive keeps the planning simple. Start with [the best adults-only resorts in Mexico](/guides/best-adults-only-resorts-in-mexico/).
- **Hawaii.** If you can add a day or two, February on Maui coincides with humpback whale season, which runs roughly December through April. Our [Hawaii couples vacation](/guides/hawaii-couples-vacation/) guide covers which island suits you.
- **The Bahamas.** Nassau is a short flight from Florida, and three- to four-night cruises from Florida ports are another option.

## Winter cabins and mountain inns

For couples who would rather trade the beach for a fireplace, February is peak cozy season. Look for an inn with a good restaurant on-site so you do not have to drive at night in winter weather.

- **The Hudson Valley, New York.** A short drive from New York City, with historic inns and farm-to-table dining. [Mohonk Mountain House](/resorts/mohonk-mountain-house/) is a classic winter choice.
- **New England.** Small towns in Vermont, New Hampshire and coastal Maine are quiet in February and feel made for long dinners.
- **The Smoky Mountains.** Cabins near Gatlinburg and Pigeon Forge are popular with couples across the Southeast; check road conditions before you go.

## Wine country in February

February is the quiet season in Napa and Sonoma. Tasting rooms are calmer, and in many years wild mustard blooms bright yellow between the vines from late winter into early spring. Book a tasting or two in advance and keep the rest of the weekend open. [Auberge du Soleil](/resorts/auberge-du-soleil/) is a splurge with valley views.

## City breaks

A city works well when you want restaurants, shows and walkable neighborhoods. New York, Las Vegas and Miami all have Valentine's menus and special performances. New Orleans adds Carnival season: in 2027, Mardi Gras is Tuesday, February 9, so parade crowds and hotel prices peak just before Valentine's weekend.

## How to book a Valentine's getaway

1. **Lock in transportation first.** Flights over a holiday weekend sell out at better prices early.
2. **Book refundable lodging if you can.** It lets you secure a room now and adjust later.
3. **Reserve dinner early.** Many restaurants serve fixed Valentine's menus and fill up weeks ahead.
4. **Consider the weekend before or after.** If the exact date is flexible, February 5–7 or February 19–21 is usually calmer and cheaper.
5. **Plan one special moment, not five.** A sunset sail, a spa appointment or a long dinner is enough. Leave the rest of the trip unscheduled.
