A great beach vacation is simple: warm sun, soft sand, clear water and nowhere you need to be. But not all beaches are created equal. Some are calm and glassy, perfect for swimming and snorkeling; others are wild and dramatic, best for walking and watching the waves. Some are lively with beach bars, and some are so quiet you'll hear nothing but the surf.

This guide covers 15 of the best beach vacations for couples in the U.S., Mexico and the Caribbean, and how to choose the right one. For broader trip planning, see our complete [romantic getaways](/) guide.

## How to choose a beach

Consider five things:

1. **Water conditions.** Do you want calm water for swimming and snorkeling, or waves?
2. **Sand.** Powder-soft white sand, golden sand, pink sand or shells?
3. **Season.** Rainy seasons, hurricane season and seaweed can affect a beach vacation.
4. **Vibe.** Quiet and secluded, or lively with restaurants and bars?
5. **Travel time.** For short trips, closer is better.

## Caribbean beach vacations

### Grace Bay, Turks and Caicos

Grace Bay on Providenciales is famous for its calm turquoise water and soft white sand, protected by a barrier reef offshore. It's an upscale, relaxed destination ideal for couples who want a pure beach vacation.

### Eagle Beach, Aruba

Aruba sits south of the main hurricane belt and enjoys reliable sunshine and trade winds. Eagle Beach is wide and uncrowded, known for its photogenic fofoti trees.

### Seven Mile Beach, Grand Cayman

Long, calm and lined with resorts and restaurants, Grand Cayman's Seven Mile Beach is great for swimming. Take a boat trip to Stingray City's sandbar.

### Trunk Bay, St. John

In the U.S. Virgin Islands, Trunk Bay is part of Virgin Islands National Park and has an underwater snorkeling trail. No passport is required for U.S. citizens.

### Anse Chastanet, St. Lucia

Backed by forested hills near the Pitons, Anse Chastanet has excellent snorkeling right off the beach. Nearby [Sugar Beach](/resorts/sugar-beach-viceroy/) sits between the twin Pitons.

## Mexico beach vacations

### Maroma Beach, Riviera Maya

Maroma Beach is one of the Riviera Maya's most admired stretches, with soft sand and calm water. [Secrets Maroma Beach](/resorts/secrets-maroma-beach/) is an adults-only all-inclusive right on it.

### Isla Mujeres

A short ferry from Cancun, Isla Mujeres has Playa Norte's shallow, calm water and a relaxed island feel. It's perfect for a day trip or a quieter stay.

Mexico's Caribbean coast can see sargassum seaweed, especially in warmer months. Ask resorts about recent conditions. Read [Mexico vacations for couples](/guides/mexico-vacations-for-couples/).

## Jamaica, the Bahamas and the Dominican Republic

### Seven Mile Beach, Negril

Negril's long beach faces west for sunsets and is lined with couples resorts and beach bars. The West End cliffs are nearby for sunset.

### Pink Sands Beach, Harbour Island

Harbour Island's beach has a soft pink hue from tiny coral fragments. The island is small, quiet and charming, with golf carts instead of cars.

### Bávaro Beach, Punta Cana

A long, palm-lined beach with many all-inclusive resorts. It's one of the best-value beach vacations in the Caribbean.

## U.S. beach vacations

### Wailea and Kaanapali, Maui

Maui's resort beaches offer calm water in the mornings, sea turtles and sunset views. [Four Seasons Resort Maui](/resorts/four-seasons-maui-wailea/) sits on Wailea Beach.

### Poipu, Kauai

Kauai's sunny south shore has swimmable coves and a relaxed pace. Conditions vary, so swim where lifeguards are present and follow posted warnings.

### Siesta Key, Florida

Siesta Key's fine white quartz sand is famous, and its Gulf water is often calm. Sunsets are a daily event.

### Bahia Honda State Park, Florida Keys

One of the prettiest beaches in the Keys, with clear water and the old Bahia Honda Rail Bridge as a backdrop. Stay in Key West at [Casa Marina](/resorts/casa-marina-key-west/) for a beach with town nearby.

### Coronado, California

Coronado's wide, golden beach in front of the Hotel del Coronado is a classic California scene. The Pacific is cooler, so it's more for walking and sunbathing than long swims.

## When to plan a beach vacation

- **Caribbean and Mexico:** December to April is driest. Late April to early June is often good value. Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30.
- **Hawaii:** Good year-round. Winter brings big waves to north shores; summer brings calmer water there.
- **Florida:** Spring and late fall are ideal; summer is hot, humid and stormy.
- **California:** Late summer and early fall are warmest, and June often brings coastal fog.

## Affordable beach vacations

Good-value beach trips include:

- **Florida's Gulf Coast** in shoulder season.
- **Gulf Shores and Orange Beach, Alabama,** for white sand at lower prices.
- **The Outer Banks, North Carolina,** in spring or fall.
- **Punta Cana** for all-inclusive value.
- **Cancun** in late spring.

## Beach vacation tips for couples

- **Go early.** Mornings are calmer, cooler and less crowded.
- **Watch the sunset together.** West-facing beaches (Gulf Coast, Negril, Maui's west side) are best.
- **Protect the reef.** Use reef-safe sunscreen and don't touch coral.
- **Respect the ocean.** Swim near lifeguards and follow posted flags.
- **Bring shade.** A beach umbrella or cabana makes long days comfortable.
- **Plan one water adventure.** Snorkeling, a sunset sail or paddleboarding.

## What to pack for a beach vacation

Two swimsuits each, a cover-up, reef-safe sunscreen, a wide-brimmed hat, polarized sunglasses, water shoes for rocky entries, a dry bag for boat trips and a good book. A lightweight long-sleeve shirt is useful for snorkeling to protect your back from the sun.

## Beach vacation styles

**The resort beach week.** A resort on the sand with pools, restaurants and loungers. Ideal for pure relaxation, especially at adults-only resorts.

**The beach town stay.** A small hotel or inn in a walkable beach town, with local restaurants and shops. Great for couples who like variety.

**The island hop.** Combine two beaches, such as Maui and Kauai, or Miami and the Florida Keys. Best with a week or more.

**The beach and culture mix.** Pair beaches with history or food, like the Riviera Maya's Mayan sites or Old San Juan's forts.

## How to spend a perfect beach day

Start with a sunrise walk while the sand is cool and empty. Swim or snorkel mid-morning, when the water is often at its calmest. Retreat to the shade for lunch and a nap during the hottest hours. Head back to the water in the late afternoon, and time your evening around the sunset, whether that's from a beach bar, a boat or a blanket on the sand.

## Beach vacation mistakes to avoid

1. **Ignoring the season.** Research rain, hurricanes and seaweed for your dates.
2. **Booking far from the sand.** A long walk or drive to the beach adds up over a week.
3. **Underestimating the sun.** Tropical sun is strong; reapply sunscreen often.
4. **Skipping water safety.** Check flags and swim near lifeguards.

## Our top three

If we had to choose: **Grace Bay** for the perfect Caribbean swim, **Wailea** for a no-passport beach with world-class resorts, and **Maroma Beach** for the easiest adults-only all-inclusive escape.
