The best couples vacations aren't the most expensive or the most photographed. They're the trips that fit both of you: the pace, the budget, the activities and the kind of downtime you each need. A beach lover and a hiker can be equally happy in Maui. A foodie and a sun-seeker can both win in Miami.

Below are 15 trips our editors recommend most often, grouped by style, with honest notes on who each one suits. For the bigger picture on planning, from budgets to timing, see our complete guide to [romantic getaways](/).

## How we chose these couples vacations

Every pick here meets four tests:

1. **It's easy to enjoy as a pair.** Logistics are manageable and the setting encourages time together.
2. **It offers variety.** There's something for different interests, so neither of you is just tagging along.
3. **It's reliable.** The destination has a strong track record for couples, with good lodging at several price points.
4. **The timing works.** We note the best seasons, since weather and crowds can make or break a trip.

## Island escapes

### 1. Maui, Hawaii

Maui is the best all-around couples vacation in the U.S. You can spend mornings snorkeling with sea turtles, afternoons by the pool and evenings at sunset dinners in Wailea. The Road to Hana adds a day of waterfalls and rainforest, and sunrise from Haleakala's summit (reservations required) is unforgettable. Stay at the adults-only [Hotel Wailea](/resorts/hotel-wailea/) for intimacy or a beachfront resort for convenience. See our [Hawaii couples vacation](/guides/hawaii-couples-vacation/) guide.

### 2. Kauai, Hawaii

Kauai suits couples who'd rather hike than shop. Take a boat along the Na Pali Coast, walk the rim of Waimea Canyon, then unwind in sunny Poipu. It's quieter than Maui and feels wonderfully remote.

### 3. St. Lucia

For a milestone, St. Lucia's twin Pitons create one of the most dramatic backdrops in the Caribbean. [Jade Mountain](/resorts/jade-mountain/) is famous for suites with no fourth wall, many with private infinity pools. Add a rainforest hike and a soak in volcanic mud baths near Soufrière. Read more in [Caribbean vacations for couples](/guides/caribbean-vacations-for-couples/).

### 4. The Bahamas

Just an hour's flight from South Florida, the Bahamas deliver impossibly blue water with minimal travel time. Stay on Cable Beach near Nassau, or island-hop to the Exumas' sandbars and Harbour Island's pink sand.

### 5. The Florida Keys

A road trip down the Overseas Highway is one of the best couples vacations in the country. Kayak mangroves in Islamorada, snorkel the reef and toast the sunset in Key West. Our guide to [Florida romantic getaways](/guides/best-romantic-getaways-in-florida/) maps it out.

## All-inclusive and adults-only escapes

### 6. Riviera Maya, Mexico

If you want the easiest possible week of relaxation, book an adults-only all-inclusive near Cancun or on the Riviera Maya. [Excellence Playa Mujeres](/resorts/excellence-playa-mujeres/) has swim-up suites and a wide choice of restaurants. Day trips to cenotes and Mayan ruins add adventure. Compare options in [the best adults-only resorts in Mexico](/guides/best-adults-only-resorts-in-mexico/).

### 7. Negril, Jamaica

Jamaica is the home of couples-only all-inclusives. Negril's Seven Mile Beach is long and relaxed, and the West End cliffs offer some of the Caribbean's best sunsets.

### 8. Punta Cana, Dominican Republic

Punta Cana offers palm-lined beaches and excellent all-inclusive value, which makes it one of the most affordable couples vacations in the Caribbean. See [all-inclusive vacations for couples](/guides/all-inclusive-vacations-for-couples/).

### 9. Los Cabos, Mexico

At the tip of Baja, Los Cabos combines desert mountains with the Pacific. It's a great pick for food-loving couples, and from roughly December to April you can often spot humpback whales from shore or on a boat tour. Note that many Cabo beaches are too rough for swimming, so choose a resort with a swimmable beach or great pools.

## Food, wine and scenery

### 10. Napa and Sonoma, California

Wine country is made for couples: tasting rooms, long lunches and countryside inns. [Auberge du Soleil](/resorts/auberge-du-soleil/) overlooks Napa Valley from an olive-covered hillside. Book tastings ahead and arrange a driver.

### 11. Big Sur, California

Highway 1 through Big Sur is one of the most beautiful drives in the world. Stay at a clifftop retreat, hike among redwoods and watch the fog roll in over the Pacific. Check road conditions before you go.

## City breaks

### 12. Las Vegas

Las Vegas is a fun couples vacation when you plan around shows, dinners, spas and desert day trips to Red Rock Canyon or Valley of Fire. Midweek stays are often better value. Read our [Las Vegas couples vacation](/guides/las-vegas-couples-vacation/) guide.

### 13. Miami

Beach mornings, Art Deco walks, Cuban coffee and rooftop nights. Miami is a great long weekend for couples who want both sun and energy.

### 14. New York City

Broadway, museums, Central Park and some of the world's best restaurants. New York is especially romantic in fall and during the December holidays.

## Quick escapes

### 15. The Hudson Valley

Just a train ride or drive from New York City, the Hudson Valley offers river towns, farm-to-table dining, hiking and historic resorts. It's perfect for anniversaries or a mid-year reset. See our [best weekend getaways from New York](/guides/best-weekend-getaways-from-new-york/).

## How to plan a couples vacation you'll both love

- **Share your must-haves.** Each of you names three things that would make the trip great.
- **Set a budget early.** Agree on a total number to avoid stress later. Our [couples vacations under $2,000](/guides/best-couples-vacations-under-2000/) guide shows how to stretch it.
- **Balance together and apart time.** A spa morning for one while the other golfs can make dinner more fun.
- **Don't overschedule.** One or two plans per day leaves room for spontaneity.
- **Pick lodging that fits your style.** All-inclusive for simplicity, a boutique hotel for character or a villa for privacy.

## Affordable couples vacations

Great couples vacations don't have to be expensive. Consider Florida's Gulf Coast in shoulder season, Las Vegas midweek, a drive-to cabin near home or an all-inclusive in Punta Cana in late spring. Flexible dates are your best tool. Read [affordable romantic getaways](/guides/affordable-romantic-getaways/) and [cheap vacation destinations](/guides/cheap-vacation-destinations/) for more ideas.

## Three sample itineraries

**A long weekend in the Florida Keys (4 nights).** Fly into Miami, drive to Islamorada for a night of kayaking and seafood, then continue to Key West for three nights of biking, snorkeling and sunset sails.

**A week on Maui (7 nights).** Spend four nights in Wailea for beaches and snorkeling, take a full day for the Road to Hana, and finish with three nights on the west side for sunsets and a whale-watching cruise in winter.

**A relaxing Mexico escape (5 nights).** Stay at an adults-only all-inclusive on the Riviera Maya, with one morning at a cenote, one afternoon at the spa and the rest of the time between the pool, the beach and dinner reservations.

## Mistakes couples make when planning vacations

1. **Choosing a destination only one person is excited about.** Compromise early, not on day three.
2. **Underestimating travel time.** Connections, transfers and time zones can turn a five-night trip into three usable days.
3. **Booking the cheapest room at a great resort.** Sometimes a smaller resort with a better room is the more romantic choice.
4. **Ignoring the season.** Research rain, heat, hurricanes and crowds for your exact dates.
5. **Planning every hour.** Leave whole afternoons open. That's where the best memories tend to happen.
6. **Forgetting the details.** Check passport validity, resort fees, what's included and cancellation terms before you pay.
