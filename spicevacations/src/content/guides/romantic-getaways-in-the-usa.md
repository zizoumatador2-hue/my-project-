You don't need a passport to find an unforgettable romantic getaway. The United States packs tropical islands, desert canyons, world-class wine regions, historic cities and dramatic coastlines into one country, and many of them are a short flight or drive from home.

This guide covers 14 of the best romantic getaways in the USA, from quick weekends to once-in-a-lifetime trips. For advice on budgets, adults-only resorts and planning, see our complete [romantic getaways](/) guide.

## Tropical and island getaways

### Maui, Hawaii

Maui is the classic no-passport honeymoon. Wailea's resort beaches are ideal for swimming and snorkeling, the Road to Hana delivers waterfalls and rainforest, and sunrise at Haleakala is breathtaking (make a reservation in advance). For privacy, the adults-only [Hotel Wailea](/resorts/hotel-wailea/) sits above the coast with ocean views from its suites. Read our full [Hawaii couples vacation](/guides/hawaii-couples-vacation/) guide.

### Kauai, Hawaii

The Garden Isle is greener and wilder. Take a Na Pali Coast boat tour, hike in Waimea Canyon and stay in Poipu on the sunny south shore. Kauai rewards couples who love nature and quiet.

### The Florida Keys

Drive the Overseas Highway from Key Largo to Key West, stopping for snorkeling, kayaking and key lime pie. In Key West, join the sunset at Mallory Square or book a sunset sail. For total seclusion, [Little Palm Island](/resorts/little-palm-island/) is an adults-only private island. See [the best romantic getaways in Florida](/guides/best-romantic-getaways-in-florida/).

### Miami

Miami blends beaches, Art Deco architecture, Latin culture and nightlife. Walk the beach at sunrise, explore Wynwood's murals and dance to live music in Little Havana. Our [Miami romantic getaways](/guides/miami-romantic-getaways/) guide has a three-day plan.

## West Coast romance

### Big Sur, California

Few places in America feel as romantic as Big Sur. Highway 1 hugs cliffs above the Pacific, with viewpoints at Bixby Creek Bridge and McWay Falls. Splurge on a clifftop stay at the adults-only [Post Ranch Inn](/resorts/post-ranch-inn/) or [Ventana Big Sur](/resorts/ventana-big-sur/). Sections of Highway 1 sometimes close for repairs, so check conditions before you go.

### Napa and Sonoma, California

Wine country is made for slow days. Schedule one or two tastings, a long lunch and a sunset dinner. [Auberge du Soleil](/resorts/auberge-du-soleil/) overlooks Napa Valley's vineyards, while Sonoma's towns feel more relaxed. Hire a driver so you can both enjoy the tastings.

### Santa Barbara, California

With Spanish Colonial architecture, beaches backed by mountains and a lively wine scene, Santa Barbara is an easy, stylish weekend from Los Angeles.

### Palm Springs, California

Mid-century modern houses, poolside cocktails and desert spa days make Palm Springs a favorite for fall-through-spring getaways.

## Desert getaways

### Sedona, Arizona

Sedona's red sandstone formations glow at sunrise and sunset. Hike to viewpoints, book a spa treatment and stargaze under clear desert skies.

### Las Vegas, Nevada

Vegas is at its most romantic when you plan beyond the casinos: a great show, dinner overlooking the Bellagio fountains, a day at Red Rock Canyon and an afternoon at the spa. See our [Las Vegas couples vacation](/guides/las-vegas-couples-vacation/) guide.

## East Coast and Southern charm

### New York City

Walk the Brooklyn Bridge at sunrise, catch a Broadway show and find a rooftop bar for sunset. New York is especially magical in fall and during the December holidays.

### The Hudson Valley, New York

A short train ride or drive from Manhattan, the Hudson Valley offers river towns, apple orchards, farm-to-table restaurants and historic stays like [Mohonk Mountain House](/resorts/mohonk-mountain-house/), a Victorian castle on a mountain lake. See the [best weekend getaways from New York](/guides/best-weekend-getaways-from-new-york/).

### Charleston, South Carolina

Charleston's pastel houses on Rainbow Row, gas-lit streets and celebrated restaurants make it one of the most romantic cities in the South. Walk along the Battery at sunset and take a carriage ride through the historic district.

### Savannah, Georgia

Savannah's oak-shaded squares draped in Spanish moss and its fountain in Forsyth Park feel like a scene from a movie. It's compact and walkable, perfect for a slow weekend.

## Mountain and lake getaways

### Lake Tahoe, California and Nevada

Lake Tahoe's clear blue water and surrounding peaks make it a year-round romantic destination. Summer brings beach days, paddleboarding and lakeside dinners; winter brings skiing and cozy cabins with fireplaces. Emerald Bay, on the lake's southwest shore, is one of the most photographed spots in California.

### Asheville and the Blue Ridge Parkway, North Carolina

In fall, the Blue Ridge Parkway is one of the country's great foliage drives. Asheville adds a creative food and craft beer scene, and the Biltmore Estate offers grand gardens and a winery to explore together.

### Vermont

For a classic New England getaway, head to Vermont in late September and October. Stay at a country inn, drive scenic backroads between covered bridges and small towns, and warm up with cider and maple treats.

## Romantic experiences worth planning ahead

Some of the best moments on a U.S. romantic getaway need advance planning:

- **Sunrise at Haleakala, Maui:** vehicle reservations are required for sunrise entry.
- **A sunset sail in Key West:** popular departures sell out, especially in winter.
- **A special dinner in Napa or Charleston:** top restaurants book up weeks ahead.
- **A Broadway show in New York:** buy tickets early for popular shows, or try the TKTS booth for same-day options.
- **A helicopter tour in Kauai or Las Vegas:** book a morning flight for calmer air and clearer views.
- **A couples massage at your resort:** reserve when you book your room, not when you arrive.

## How to choose the right U.S. getaway

**Match distance to time.** For two or three nights, stay within about three hours of home. Save Hawaii for trips of a week or more.

**Think about the season.** Florida and the Southwest are best in spring and fall; the Northeast shines in fall; Hawaii is pleasant year-round with quieter shoulder seasons.

**Choose the vibe.** Beaches and relaxation (Florida, Hawaii), food and wine (Napa, Charleston), scenery (Big Sur, Sedona) or energy (New York, Las Vegas, Miami).

**Budget wisely.** Some of the most romantic trips are also affordable. See [affordable romantic getaways](/guides/affordable-romantic-getaways/) for ideas.

## Tips for a romantic trip in the USA

- **Bring the right ID.** Domestic flights require a REAL ID-compliant license or another accepted ID.
- **Book the special things early.** Sunrise at Haleakala, popular restaurants and weekend inns fill up.
- **Plan around golden hour.** Sunset sails, beach walks and scenic drives are best in the late afternoon.
- **Leave space.** The most romantic moments usually happen in the unplanned hours.

## Where to stay for romance

The right place to stay can make an ordinary trip feel special. Look for adults-only or couples-focused properties when you want quiet, rooms with a balcony or view when you'll spend time in them, and walkable locations in cities so you can stroll to dinner. In wine country and the mountains, small inns often feel more personal than large resorts, and historic hotels add a sense of occasion to anniversaries. Whatever you choose, read recent guest reviews on the booking site for noise, cleanliness and service before you commit.
