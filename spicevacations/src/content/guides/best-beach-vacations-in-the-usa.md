The United States has some of the world's most beautiful beaches, and you don't need a passport to reach any of them. From Hawaii's volcanic coastlines to Florida's powder-soft sand, California's golden surf beaches and New England's windswept dunes, there's a beach vacation for every season and style.

This guide ranks 16 of the best beach vacations in the USA for couples, grouped by region, with the best time to go and where to stay. For more on planning trips for two, see our complete [romantic getaways](/) guide.

## Hawaii

### Wailea, Maui

Wailea is Maui's resort coast, with a string of golden crescent beaches that are often calm in the morning. It's excellent for swimming and snorkeling, and the coastal walking path connects the resorts. [Four Seasons Resort Maui at Wailea](/resorts/four-seasons-maui-wailea/) sits on Wailea Beach and has an adults-only pool.

### Kaanapali, Maui

On Maui's west side, Kaanapali's long beach is lined with resorts and faces the sunset. Snorkeling near Black Rock at the north end is popular. West Maui communities have been rebuilding after the 2023 wildfires, so travel respectfully and support local businesses.

### Poipu, Kauai

Kauai's sunny south shore offers swimmable coves and a chance to see Hawaiian monk seals resting on the sand (keep your distance). It's quieter than Maui.

### Waikiki, Oahu

Waikiki combines a lively beach with Honolulu's restaurants and culture. Watch the sunset with Hawaiian music at [Halekulani](/resorts/halekulani/)'s House Without a Key.

### Hapuna Beach, Hawaii Island

One of the Big Island's largest white-sand beaches, with calm conditions in summer.

## Florida

### Bahia Honda State Park, Florida Keys

Clear water, soft sand and the historic rail bridge make Bahia Honda one of the prettiest beaches in the Keys.

### Key West

Key West's beaches are small, but the town's sunsets, restaurants and energy make it a beach vacation favorite. [Casa Marina](/resorts/casa-marina-key-west/) has one of the island's largest private beaches.

### Siesta Key

Siesta Key's fine white quartz sand is famously soft, and the Gulf water is often calm.

### St. Pete Beach

A wide, sandy beach and great sunsets less than an hour from Tampa's airport, depending on traffic. [The Don CeSar](/resorts/the-don-cesar/), the "Pink Palace," is the landmark.

### The Emerald Coast

Destin and the towns along Scenic Highway 30A have sugar-white sand and green-tinted water. Spring and fall are less crowded than summer.

### Miami Beach

Sunrise walks, Art Deco hotels and nightlife. It's the best choice if you want beach time plus a city. Read [Miami romantic getaways](/guides/miami-romantic-getaways/).

For more, see [the best romantic getaways in Florida](/guides/best-romantic-getaways-in-florida/).

## California

### Coronado, San Diego

Coronado's wide golden beach, in front of the historic Hotel del Coronado, is one of the most classic beach scenes in California.

### Laguna Beach

Rocky coves, tide pools, art galleries and cliffside restaurants make Laguna Beach especially romantic. Crescent Bay and Victoria Beach are favorites.

Pacific water in California is cooler than in Florida or Hawaii, so expect beach walks and sunbathing more than long swims.

## The Southeast and Northeast

### Hilton Head, South Carolina

Wide, hard-packed beaches perfect for biking, plus golf, seafood and a relaxed island feel.

### The Outer Banks, North Carolina

Barrier islands with tall dunes, lighthouses and wild beaches. Visit Jockey's Ridge State Park for sunset on the largest natural sand dune system on the East Coast.

### Cape Cod, Massachusetts

Classic New England: shingled cottages, lobster shacks and the Cape Cod National Seashore's dunes and beaches. The water is cool, so July through September is best.

## Choosing the right U.S. beach

- **Warmest water:** Hawaii and South Florida.
- **Best sunsets:** Florida's Gulf Coast, Key West and Maui's west side.
- **Best snorkeling:** Hawaii and the Florida Keys.
- **Best for a beach plus a city:** Miami Beach, Waikiki and San Diego.
- **Best for quiet charm:** Cape Cod, the Outer Banks and Sanibel.

## When to go

- **Hawaii:** year-round; spring and fall are quieter.
- **Florida:** March to May and October to November. Summer brings heat and afternoon storms, and hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30.
- **California:** late summer and early fall are warmest; early summer can bring coastal fog.
- **The Carolinas and New England:** summer and early fall.

## Beach safety tips

- Swim near lifeguards and follow posted flags.
- Learn how to escape a rip current by swimming parallel to shore.
- Use reef-safe sunscreen and don't touch coral or wildlife.
- Stay hydrated and seek shade at midday.

## Sample weekend: a Gulf Coast beach escape

**Friday:** Fly into Tampa and drive to St. Pete Beach. Catch the sunset from the sand and have dinner by the water. **Saturday:** Rent paddleboards, relax by the pool and drive to Pass-a-Grille for sunset. **Sunday:** Walk the beach at sunrise and explore downtown St. Petersburg's waterfront before your flight.

## Hidden-gem U.S. beaches for couples

- **Hanalei Bay, Kauai:** a sweeping crescent beneath green mountains, calmest in summer.
- **Anna Maria Island, Florida:** Old Florida charm, no high-rises and slow, sunny days.
- **Sanibel Island, Florida:** famous for shelling and wildlife refuges, with a quiet, low-key feel.
- **Cannon Beach, Oregon:** dramatic Haystack Rock, tide pools and moody Pacific sunsets. The water is cold, but the scenery is spectacular.
- **Pfeiffer Beach, Big Sur:** purple-tinged sand and a rock arch framing the sunset. Check access conditions before you go.
- **Tybee Island, Georgia:** a laid-back beach town a short drive from historic Savannah.

## Where to stay for a romantic beach trip

Choose oceanfront if you can; hearing the waves from your room is part of the magic. In Hawaii and Florida, resorts with adults-only pools give couples a quiet refuge. In small beach towns, boutique inns and cottages within walking distance of the sand often feel more romantic than large hotels. Whatever you choose, check parking, resort fees and beach access policies, which vary widely.

## Best U.S. beaches by couple type

**Honeymooners:** Wailea on Maui or a private island like Little Palm Island in the Keys. **Budget travelers:** St. Pete Beach, Gulf Shores or the Outer Banks outside summer. **Foodies:** Miami Beach and Laguna Beach. **Adventurers:** Kauai's Poipu and Hanalei. **Nature lovers:** Cape Cod National Seashore and Bahia Honda State Park.

## Sample week: Maui beaches

**Days 1–3 in Wailea:** Snorkel at the calm coves in the morning, lounge by the pool in the afternoon and watch the sunset from the coastal path. **Day 4:** Drive the Road to Hana, stopping at black-sand Waianapanapa State Park (reservations required). **Days 5–7 on the west side:** Move to Kaanapali or Kapalua for snorkeling at Black Rock, a sunset sail, and, in winter, whale watching from the beach.

## Beach towns worth a detour

Some of the best U.S. beach vacations are as much about the town as the sand. Carmel-by-the-Sea pairs a white-sand beach with storybook cottages and galleries. Key West adds historic streets and lively sunset celebrations. Seaside, on Florida's Scenic Highway 30A, is a pastel planned town with bike paths and beach pavilions. Chatham on Cape Cod has a working fish pier where you can watch seals and fishing boats come in.
