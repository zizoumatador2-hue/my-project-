New York City is one of the most romantic cities in the world, but sometimes the most romantic thing you can do is leave it. Within a few hours by car or train, you can be in a river town surrounded by mountains, on a windswept beach, in a Gilded Age seaside city or at a Victorian castle beside a lake.

This guide covers 14 of the best romantic weekend getaways from New York, with travel times and ideas for each. For more escapes across the country, see [weekend getaways for couples](/guides/weekend-getaways-for-couples/), and for broader planning, our complete [romantic getaways](/) guide.

## The Hudson Valley

### Beacon and Cold Spring

Just over an hour by train from Grand Central, Beacon is home to Dia Beacon, a contemporary art museum in a former factory, along with a lively Main Street of cafés and shops. Nearby Cold Spring is a charming river village with antiques and access to hiking trails with Hudson River views. Both are easy car-free escapes.

### Hudson and Rhinebeck

Further north, the city of Hudson has a long main street full of antique shops, galleries and excellent restaurants. Rhinebeck offers historic inns, farm stands and a classic small-town feel.

### New Paltz and Mohonk Mountain House

New Paltz sits beneath the Shawangunk Ridge, famous for hiking and rock climbing. Nearby, [Mohonk Mountain House](/resorts/mohonk-mountain-house/) is a Victorian castle on a mountain lake, with miles of trails, gazebos and a spa. It's one of the most romantic places to stay in New York State.

## The Catskills

The Catskills offer mountain views, waterfalls and cozy lodges with fireplaces. Towns like Phoenicia, Woodstock and Livingston Manor have creative restaurants and a laid-back vibe. Hike to Kaaterskill Falls, one of the tallest waterfalls in New York, or simply relax by a fire.

## Long Island

### The North Fork

The North Fork is Long Island's quieter, agricultural side, with wineries, farm stands and bay-side sunsets. It's ideal for a relaxed wine weekend.

### The Hamptons

The Hamptons are famous for beautiful ocean beaches, charming villages and summer glamour. Visit in late spring or early fall for fewer crowds and lower rates.

### Montauk

At the eastern tip of Long Island, Montauk is a laid-back surf town with wide beaches, the historic Montauk Point Lighthouse and fresh seafood.

## New England

### Mystic, Connecticut

Mystic is a picturesque seaside village with a historic seaport museum, a drawbridge and seafood restaurants. It's an easy, charming weekend.

### Litchfield Hills, Connecticut

Rolling countryside, country inns, antique shops and vineyards make the Litchfield Hills a peaceful escape, especially in fall.

### Newport, Rhode Island

Newport's Gilded Age mansions, the Cliff Walk along the Atlantic and sailing on Narragansett Bay make it a romantic classic. Stroll the harbor at sunset and dine on fresh seafood.

### The Berkshires, Massachusetts

The Berkshires blend culture and nature: summer concerts at Tanglewood, museums like the Clark Art Institute and MASS MoCA, and spectacular fall foliage.

## Mountains and lakes

### Lake Placid

A longer drive, but worth it for mountain scenery, lakeside walks and Olympic history. Lake Placid is lovely in summer, fall and winter.

## Beaches and cities

### Cape May, New Jersey

Cape May's Victorian houses, gaslit streets and beaches make it one of the most romantic seaside towns on the East Coast. Watch the sunset at Sunset Beach.

### Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

An easy train ride away, Philadelphia offers history, museums, a thriving food scene and charming neighborhoods like Old City and Rittenhouse Square.

## Romantic weekend getaways by season

- **Spring:** Hudson Valley gardens, Philadelphia, Mystic.
- **Summer:** The Hamptons, Montauk, the North Fork, Cape May, the Berkshires for Tanglewood.
- **Fall:** Hudson Valley, Catskills, Litchfield Hills, the Berkshires, Lake Placid for foliage.
- **Winter:** Cozy Catskills lodges, Mohonk Mountain House, Lake Placid for snow.

## Couples trips from NYC without a car

Train-friendly escapes include Beacon, Cold Spring and Hudson (Metro-North or Amtrak), Philadelphia (Amtrak or NJ Transit), and the Hamptons and Montauk (Long Island Rail Road). Bike rentals and rideshares help once you arrive.

## A sample Hudson Valley weekend

**Friday:** Take an afternoon train to Beacon, check into an inn and have dinner on Main Street.
**Saturday:** Visit Dia Beacon in the morning, hike to a river viewpoint in the afternoon and enjoy a farm-to-table dinner.
**Sunday:** Take the train to Cold Spring for brunch and antiques, then head home.

## Tips for weekend getaways from New York

- **Leave early or late** to avoid Friday traffic.
- **Book early for fall foliage weekends**, which sell out.
- **Take the train** when possible; it's relaxing and avoids parking.
- **Plan one special dinner** and keep the rest flexible.

## Where to stay

The style of lodging shapes a weekend away. In the Hudson Valley and Catskills, look for historic inns, restored farmhouses and design-forward lodges with fireplaces. On Long Island's East End, boutique hotels and bed-and-breakfasts put you close to beaches and villages. In Newport and Cape May, Victorian inns and harbor-view hotels add to the romance. Book early for summer weekends at the beach and fall weekends in the mountains, when the best places sell out weeks or even months ahead.

## Romantic experiences near New York

- **Apple and pumpkin picking** at Hudson Valley orchards in the fall.
- **Wine tasting** on the North Fork or in the Hudson Valley.
- **A sunset sail** on Narragansett Bay in Newport.
- **Hiking to a waterfall** like Kaaterskill Falls in the Catskills.
- **A concert under the stars** at Tanglewood in the Berkshires in summer.
- **A carriage or trolley tour** through Cape May's Victorian streets.
- **A spa day** at a mountain resort such as Mohonk.

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Leaving at 5 p.m. on a Friday.** Traffic out of the city can double your travel time; leave early or take the train.
2. **Forgetting to book dinner.** Popular restaurants in small towns fill up on weekends.
3. **Trying to do too much.** Pick one base and explore nearby.
4. **Ignoring the season.** Some beach towns are sleepy in winter, and some mountain lodges close between seasons.

## A sample Newport weekend

**Friday:** Drive to Newport and check into a harbor-view inn. Have seafood by the water. **Saturday:** Walk the Cliff Walk in the morning, tour a Gilded Age mansion in the afternoon and take a sunset sail. **Sunday:** Drive Ocean Drive's scenic loop, have brunch and head home.

## Weekend budget tips

Weekends away from New York don't have to be expensive. Travel on Sunday to Tuesday for lower rates at many inns, take the train to skip gas, tolls and parking, and look at shoulder seasons, such as the Hamptons in June or September. Pack a picnic from a local farm stand for one meal, and save your splurge for a special dinner or spa treatment.

## Surprise-weekend idea

Planning a surprise? Book a train to Beacon or Hudson, reserve a cozy inn and dinner, and pack a bag for your partner. Tell them only to meet you at Grand Central. The short trip up the river, with views of the Hudson Highlands, is part of the gift, and the whole trip takes less than a day of planning.
