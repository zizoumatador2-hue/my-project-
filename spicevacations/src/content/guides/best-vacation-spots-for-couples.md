Choosing a vacation spot as a couple is easier when you start with how you like to travel rather than where. Two people who love long lunches and wine will be happier in Napa than at a party resort. A couple who can't sit still will love Kauai more than a week on a lounger.

This guide matches the best vacation spots for couples to five travel styles. If you'd like the full planning playbook, including budgets and seasons, read our complete guide to [romantic getaways](/).

## Find your couple travel style

Answer these quickly:

1. Would you rather spend a day on the beach, or exploring?
2. Is dinner the highlight of your day, or just fuel?
3. Do you want nightlife, or are you asleep by ten?
4. Is this a milestone, like a honeymoon or big anniversary?

Your answers point to one of the styles below.

## For beach lovers

If your perfect day involves warm water and a good book, these are the best vacation spots for couples.

### Maui, Hawaii

Wailea's golden beaches are calm and swimmable, especially in the morning. [Four Seasons Resort Maui at Wailea](/resorts/four-seasons-maui-wailea/) has an adults-only Serenity Pool that's a favorite for couples. Snorkel with sea turtles, then watch the sunset over neighboring islands.

### Turks and Caicos

Grace Bay on Providenciales is celebrated for its calm turquoise water and powdery sand. It's a pure beach vacation with excellent snorkeling close to shore.

### The Riviera Maya, Mexico

Just south of Cancun, beaches like Maroma are known for soft sand and clear water. [Secrets Maroma Beach](/resorts/secrets-maroma-beach/) is an adults-only all-inclusive right on it. Read [Mexico vacations for couples](/guides/mexico-vacations-for-couples/).

### Siesta Key, Florida

Siesta Key's fine quartz sand is famous, and Gulf sunsets are a daily show. It's an easy, no-passport beach escape.

## For foodies

For couples who plan trips around restaurants, these are the best destinations.

### Napa and Sonoma, California

Wine tastings, farm-to-table lunches and some of America's most celebrated restaurants. Stay in the heart of the valley and plan one special dinner.

### Charleston, South Carolina

Lowcountry cuisine, oyster bars and cocktail lounges in a gorgeous historic setting.

### New York City

From tiny West Village bistros to Michelin-starred tasting menus, New York is a food lover's dream. Pair dinner with a Broadway show.

### Miami

Cuban cafés, Peruvian ceviche and waterfront dining. Our [Miami romantic getaways](/guides/miami-romantic-getaways/) guide includes where to eat.

## For adventurers

Couples who want to hike, snorkel and explore will love these spots.

### Kauai, Hawaii

Boat tours along the Na Pali Coast, canyon hikes and river kayaking make Kauai Hawaii's adventure island.

### Hawaii Island (the Big Island)

Walk through volcanic landscapes in Hawaii Volcanoes National Park, snorkel with manta rays at night and stargaze from Mauna Kea's slopes.

### Los Cabos, Mexico

Kayak to El Arco, hike desert trails and watch for humpback whales in winter.

### St. Lucia

Climb the Gros Piton (with a local guide), hike rainforest trails and bathe in volcanic mud near Soufrière. [Sugar Beach, A Viceroy Resort](/resorts/sugar-beach-viceroy/) sits between the Pitons with a beach for recovering afterward.

## For night owls

If you like to dress up and stay out late, these cities deliver.

### Las Vegas

Headline shows, rooftop bars and some of the best restaurants in the country, with desert adventures by day. See our [Las Vegas couples vacation](/guides/las-vegas-couples-vacation/) guide.

### Miami

Rooftop lounges, salsa clubs and beach parties, plus a relaxed morning on the sand.

### New Orleans

Live jazz on Frenchmen Street, historic bars and unforgettable Creole cooking make New Orleans a lively couples getaway.

## For honeymooners and milestones

For the trips that matter most, choose places with jaw-dropping settings and exceptional service.

### St. Lucia

The Pitons, private plunge pools and rooms with no fourth wall. Few places feel as romantic.

### Maui

A no-passport honeymoon with everything: beaches, volcanoes, waterfalls and luxury resorts.

### Adults-only resorts in Mexico

The easiest honeymoon: swim-up suites, many restaurants and nothing to plan. See [the best adults-only resorts in Mexico](/guides/best-adults-only-resorts-in-mexico/).

## For wellness seekers

Some couples come home from vacation more tired than when they left. If your goal is to genuinely recharge, choose a destination built around rest, movement and good food.

### Sedona, Arizona

Sedona's red-rock landscape feels restorative on its own. Mornings are for easy hikes to viewpoints; afternoons are for spa treatments, yoga and long lunches with a view. The dry climate and clear skies also make it one of the best places in the country for stargazing together.

### Lanai, Hawaii

Lanai is Hawaii's quiet island. In its cool upland forests, [Sensei Lanai](/resorts/sensei-lanai/) is an adults-focused wellness retreat with guided programs and private spa hale. It's the opposite of a busy resort vacation, and that's the point.

### Palm Springs, California

Palm Springs is built for lounging. Book a hotel with a great pool, schedule a couples massage and spend evenings at restaurants in restored mid-century buildings. Visit between fall and spring to avoid extreme summer heat.

## For culture and history lovers

Travel can be romantic when it tells a story. These destinations reward curious couples.

### Santa Fe, New Mexico

Adobe architecture, art galleries along Canyon Road and a distinctive cuisine built on red and green chile. Santa Fe's high-desert light is famous among painters, and its evenings are cool and clear.

### Old San Juan, Puerto Rico

Blue cobblestone streets, pastel colonial buildings and centuries-old forts overlooking the Atlantic. Puerto Rico requires no passport for U.S. citizens, and El Yunque National Forest's rainforest trails are an easy day trip.

### The Yucatán Peninsula, Mexico

Combine a beach resort on the Riviera Maya with visits to Mayan sites such as Tulum's clifftop ruins or Chichén Itzá, and swims in freshwater cenotes. Start early to avoid heat and crowds.

### Savannah and Charleston

The South's historic cities combine architecture, gardens, ghost stories and exceptional food. Both are compact enough to explore on foot, hand in hand.

## Sample one-week trips by style

**The beach lovers' week.** Fly to Cancun and spend seven nights at an adults-only resort on the Riviera Maya. Take one day trip to a cenote and one sunset sail; spend the rest of the time by the water.

**The foodies' week.** Start with three nights in San Francisco's restaurant scene, then drive to Napa and Sonoma for four nights of tastings, farm lunches and one splurge dinner.

**The adventurers' week.** Spend seven nights on Kauai: a Na Pali Coast boat tour, a Waimea Canyon hike, a kayak trip up the Wailua River and beach afternoons in Poipu.

**The milestone week.** Fly to St. Lucia for a week split between a hillside hideaway with Piton views and a beach resort, with a rainforest hike and a catamaran day in between.

## How much time do you need?

A good rule: the farther you travel, the longer you should stay. Plan at least three nights for destinations within a short flight, five nights for Mexico and the Caribbean, and seven nights or more for Hawaii from the East Coast. Short trips to faraway places often feel rushed and tiring, which is the opposite of romantic.

## Underrated vacation spots for couples

If you've done the classics, consider these less obvious picks:

- **Anna Maria Island, Florida:** a laid-back Gulf island with no high-rises and a slower pace.
- **Door County, Wisconsin:** lighthouses, cherry orchards and small harbor towns on Lake Michigan.
- **Santa Barbara wine country:** rolling vineyards around Los Olivos and Santa Ynez, less crowded than Napa.
- **Isla Mujeres, Mexico:** a short ferry ride from Cancun, with golf carts, calm beaches and a relaxed island vibe.
- **Eleuthera, Bahamas:** a long, quiet island with empty beaches and very few crowds.

## Tips for choosing together

- **Set a budget first**, then shortlist destinations that fit it.
- **Consider travel time.** Long flights are worth it for trips of a week or more.
- **Check entry requirements.** Mexico and most Caribbean islands require a passport.
- **Alternate choices.** If one person picked last time, let the other choose this time.
