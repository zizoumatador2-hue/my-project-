An anniversary trip does not have to be big to feel meaningful. What matters is that it fits the two of you: the pace you enjoy, the time you can take off and the kind of moment you want to remember. Below are ideas organized by milestone, by trip length and by style, with practical tips for booking.

## Anniversary ideas by milestone

Some couples like to tie the trip to the traditional anniversary themes. It is a fun way to pick a destination or plan a small surprise.

| Anniversary | Traditional theme | Trip idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | Paper | A weekend at a nearby inn, with a handwritten itinerary as the gift |
| 5th | Wood | A cabin in the mountains or a lodge in a national park |
| 10th | Tin or aluminum | A first trip together to Hawaii or the Caribbean |
| 15th | Crystal | Clear-water snorkeling in the Bahamas, Turks and Caicos or Maui |
| 20th | China | A food-focused trip, such as wine country or a city with great restaurants |
| 25th | Silver | A bucket-list stay, such as St. Lucia's Pitons or an overwater villa |
| 30th and beyond | Pearl, ruby, gold | Return to your honeymoon spot, or take the trip you always postponed |

## Ideas by trip length

**Two or three nights.** Choose a place you can reach in a few hours. Beach towns in the off-season, wine country, historic cities like Charleston and Savannah, and mountain inns all work well. Our [weekend getaways for couples](/guides/weekend-getaways-for-couples/) has 20 drive-to and short-flight ideas.

**Four or five nights.** A short nonstop flight opens up Florida, Mexico's Caribbean coast and the Bahamas. An all-inclusive resort removes daily planning, which helps on a shorter trip. Compare options in [the best all-inclusive resorts for couples](/guides/best-all-inclusive-resorts-for-couples/).

**A week or more.** This is when longer flights are worth it. Hawaii, St. Lucia and Antigua reward a slower pace. [Four Seasons Maui at Wailea](/resorts/four-seasons-maui-wailea/) and [Jade Mountain](/resorts/jade-mountain/) are two special-occasion favorites.

## Ideas by style

- **Beach and sunsets.** Florida's Gulf Coast, the Florida Keys, Aruba and Maui. On the Gulf Coast, [The Don CeSar](/resorts/the-don-cesar/) is a classic for a celebratory dinner.
- **Food and wine.** Napa and Sonoma, the Texas Hill Country or a long weekend of restaurant reservations in New York or New Orleans.
- **Scenery and quiet.** Big Sur's coastline, Sedona's red rocks or a lakeside lodge. [Ventana Big Sur](/resorts/ventana-big-sur/) is adults-only and set among redwoods above the Pacific.
- **Adventure together.** Snorkeling, a helicopter tour on Kauai or hiking in a national park. Shared challenges often become the best stories.
- **Revisit your honeymoon.** Going back, even for a few nights, is one of the most meaningful anniversary trips. See [the best honeymoon resorts](/guides/best-honeymoon-resorts/) for places couples love to return to.

## Making the trip feel like an anniversary

1. **Tell the hotel, but do not count on perks.** A note in the booking and a mention at check-in is enough.
2. **Plan one marked moment.** A dinner with a view, a sunset sail or a couples spa treatment. One is enough.
3. **Keep the rest unscheduled.** Free time is what makes a short trip feel restful.
4. **Bring something small.** A photo from your wedding or first trip, or a gift that matches the anniversary theme.

## About "anniversary packages"

Hotels and resorts often advertise anniversary or romance packages. Before you book one, compare the package price with the room rate plus the extras you would actually use.
