An anniversary trip does not have to be big to feel meaningful. What matters is that it fits the two of you: the pace you enjoy, the time you can take off and the kind of moment you want to remember. Below are ideas organized by milestone, by trip length and by style, with practical tips for booking.

## Anniversary ideas by milestone

Some couples like to tie the trip to the traditional anniversary themes. It is a fun way to pick a destination or plan a small surprise.

| Anniversary | Traditional theme | Trip idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | Paper | A weekend at a nearby inn, with a handwritten itinerary as the gift |
| 5th | Wood | A cabin in the mountains or a lodge in a national park |
| 10th | Tin or aluminum | A first trip together to Hawaii or the Caribbean |
| 15th | Crystal | Clear-water snorkeling in the Bahamas, Turks and Caicos or Maui |
| 20th | China | A food-focused trip, such as wine country or a city with great restaurants |
| 25th | Silver | A bucket-list stay, such as St. Lucia's Pitons or an overwater villa |
| 30th and beyond | Pearl, ruby, gold | Return to your honeymoon spot, or take the trip you always postponed |

## Ideas by trip length

**Two or three nights.** Choose a place you can reach in a few hours. Beach towns in the off-season, wine country, historic cities like Charleston and Savannah, and mountain inns all work well. Our [weekend getaways for couples](/guides/weekend-getaways-for-couples/) has 20 drive-to and short-flight ideas.

**Four or five nights.** A short nonstop flight opens up Florida, Mexico's Caribbean coast and the Bahamas. An all-inclusive resort removes daily planning, which helps on a shorter trip. Compare options in [the best all-inclusive resorts for couples](/guides/best-all-inclusive-resorts-for-couples/).

**A week or more.** This is when longer flights are worth it. Hawaii, St. Lucia and Antigua reward a slower pace. [Four Seasons Maui at Wailea](/resorts/four-seasons-maui-wailea/) and [Jade Mountain](/resorts/jade-mountain/) are two special-occasion favorites.

## Ideas by style

- **Beach and sunsets.** Florida's Gulf Coast, the Florida Keys, Aruba and Maui. On the Gulf Coast, [The Don CeSar](/resorts/the-don-cesar/) is a classic for a celebratory dinner.
- **Food and wine.** Napa and Sonoma, the Texas Hill Country or a long weekend of restaurant reservations in New York or New Orleans.
- **Scenery and quiet.** Big Sur's coastline, Sedona's red rocks or a lakeside lodge. [Ventana Big Sur](/resorts/ventana-big-sur/) is adults-only and set among redwoods above the Pacific.
- **Adventure together.** Snorkeling, a helicopter tour on Kauai or hiking in a national park. Shared challenges often become the best stories.
- **Revisit your honeymoon.** Going back, even for a few nights, is one of the most meaningful anniversary trips. See [the best honeymoon resorts](/guides/best-honeymoon-resorts/) for places couples love to return to.

## Making the trip feel like an anniversary

1. **Tell the hotel, but do not count on perks.** A note in the booking and a mention at check-in is enough.
2. **Plan one marked moment.** A dinner with a view, a sunset sail or a couples spa treatment. One is enough.
3. **Keep the rest unscheduled.** Free time is what makes a short trip feel restful.
4. **Bring something small.** A photo from your wedding or first trip, or a gift that matches the anniversary theme.

## About "anniversary packages"

Hotels and resorts often advertise anniversary or romance packages. Before you book one, compare the package price with the room rate plus the extras you would actually use. Sometimes the package is good value; sometimes it bundles things you would not choose. Our guide to [how to find vacation deals](/guides/how-to-find-vacation-deals/) explains how to compare.

## Choosing the right length for your anniversary

Length matters more than most couples expect. A short trip is easiest to fit around work and family, but it leaves less time to unwind, so plan for arrival and departure days that are not packed with tasks. A few guidelines:

- **Two or three nights.** Pick a place within a short flight or drive, such as a city with a strong dinner scene or a nearby wine region. Avoid long connections; they eat up the trip.
- **Four to six nights.** This is the sweet spot for most couples. It gives you one full day of exploring, one day of doing nothing and a special dinner, without a long stretch of travel.
- **A week or more.** Longer trips justify international flights and resort time with fewer transfers. Build in one or two rest days so the schedule does not feel like a checklist.

Whatever length you choose, book the flights and the first night before you book anything optional. Those two decisions shape the rest of the trip.

## Setting a budget that feels good

Anniversary trips often become more expensive than planned because they feel like an occasion. A simple budget keeps the focus on the time together:

1. **Decide the total first.** Include flights, lodging, food, transportation and one splurge.
2. **Spend on the moment you will remember.** A sunset cruise, a spa treatment or a tasting menu usually matters more than an upgraded room you will only sleep in.
3. **Compare the all-in cost.** Resorts with meals included can look expensive but may cost less overall than paying for every meal separately.
4. **Watch for hidden fees.** Resort fees, parking and taxes can add up. Check the total before you commit.

Rates change by season and day of the week, so compare a few dates. Midweek stays and shoulder seasons often cost less, and they are usually quieter too. Our guide to [how to find vacation deals](/guides/how-to-find-vacation-deals/) walks through the process.

## Planning the anniversary moment

The best anniversary trips usually include one planned highlight and plenty of open time. Ideas that work well for many couples:

- A dinner with a view, booked a few weeks ahead for a weekend or holiday date.
- A sunrise hike or a short boat trip followed by a late breakfast.
- A spa appointment for two, scheduled for the afternoon after a busy morning.
- A cooking class or a tasting tour that gives you something to talk about later.

Share your plans before you go. If one of you wants more adventure and the other wants more rest, alternate the days so both of you get what you need.

## Making it easier on the day of travel

Anniversary trips start with a long travel day more often than not. Reduce the friction: book a nonstop flight when you can, pack a small bag with essentials for the first evening, and leave a buffer of at least an hour between arrival and your first reservation. If you are flying into a new time zone, a light first day usually works better than trying to see everything at once.

## Anniversary trips by budget

**Modest budget.** Drive-to destinations work well here. A lakeside lodge, a historic inn or a weekend in a nearby city can feel special without a long flight. Stay midweek, cook one dinner together and save the splurge for a single evening out.

**Mid-range budget.** At this level you can add a short flight and a few nights in a resort or boutique hotel. Look for packages that include breakfast, and compare them against the room rate before you book.

**Higher budgets.** A destination trip with flights, a luxury stay and a private experience such as a sailing charter or a private chef dinner is realistic. Plan these well in advance, because the best rooms and experiences sell out first.

## Questions to ask before you book

A short list of questions can prevent surprises on an anniversary trip:

- Does the rate include breakfast, and is it served at a time that suits you?
- Is there an age minimum, and does it apply to every guest?
- What is the cancellation policy, and when does it take effect?
- Can the property note your anniversary in the reservation?
- Are there resort fees or service charges that are not in the quoted rate?

Write down the answers and keep the confirmation email handy. If anything is unclear, call the property directly rather than relying on a booking website summary.

## Ideas for a first anniversary

The first anniversary is a good time for a trip that feels like a fresh start. Choose a place you have talked about but never visited, or return to the city where you met. Keep it simple: two or three nights, one nice dinner and a morning walk that leads to a favorite coffee shop. The point is to celebrate the year, not to prove anything.

## Ideas for a milestone anniversary

Milestone anniversaries, such as the 10th or 25th, are the right time for a longer trip or a more ambitious itinerary. Consider a multi-stop route that connects two regions, such as a city and a coastline, or a trip that includes a cooking class, a guided hike or a spa week. If other family members want to join, plan a separate day for the two of you so the anniversary still feels personal.
