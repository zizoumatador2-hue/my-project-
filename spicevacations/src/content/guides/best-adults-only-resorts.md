Adults-only resorts are one of the easiest upgrades you can make to a vacation. Without kids' clubs, water slides or family pool games, the atmosphere shifts toward long lunches, spa afternoons, sunset cocktails and quiet conversation. They're especially popular for honeymoons, anniversaries and any couple who simply wants a break.

## Best adults-only resorts in Mexico

### Excellence Playa Mujeres

North of Cancun's busy Hotel Zone, [Excellence Playa Mujeres](/resorts/excellence-playa-mujeres/) is a sprawling, polished adults-only all-inclusive with winding pools, swim-up suites and a wide choice of restaurants. It's a dependable pick for first-time all-inclusive travelers and honeymooners.

### Secrets Maroma Beach Riviera Cancun

Maroma Beach is one of the Riviera Maya's most admired stretches of sand, and [Secrets Maroma Beach](/resorts/secrets-maroma-beach/) sits right on it. It's ideal for couples who want a classic all-inclusive with an exceptional beach.

### Hotel Xcaret Arte

[Hotel Xcaret Arte](/resorts/hotel-xcaret-arte/) celebrates Mexican art and culture with workshops, design-driven spaces and packages that have included access to the group's nearby parks. It's a great choice for curious couples who want more than a pool.

### Le Blanc Spa Resort Cancun

Known for butler service and a large spa, [Le Blanc Spa Resort Cancun](/resorts/le-blanc-spa-resort-cancun/) offers an elegant, quiet atmosphere in the heart of the Hotel Zone.

### Breathless Cabo San Lucas

For social couples, [Breathless Cabo San Lucas](/resorts/breathless-cabo-san-lucas/) brings rooftop pool views and an upbeat vibe to Medano Beach, a short walk from the marina.

For a deeper comparison, read [the best adults-only resorts in Mexico](/guides/best-adults-only-resorts-in-mexico/).

## Best adults-only resorts in Jamaica and the Bahamas

### Couples Negril

On calm Bloody Bay, [Couples Negril](/resorts/couples-negril/) is relaxed and friendly, with water sports and easy access to Negril's famous sunsets.

### Couples Swept Away

On Seven Mile Beach, [Couples Swept Away](/resorts/couples-swept-away/) suits active couples, with sports facilities and fitness options set among lush gardens.

### Sandals Montego Bay

Minutes from Montego Bay's airport, [Sandals Montego Bay](/resorts/sandals-montego-bay/) makes arrival easy and is ideal for shorter trips.

### Sandals Royal Bahamian

On Nassau's Cable Beach, [Sandals Royal Bahamian](/resorts/sandals-royal-bahamian/) adds a private offshore island for guests, reached by a short boat ride.

## Best adults-only resorts in the Dominican Republic and Antigua

### Excellence Punta Cana

On the quieter Uvero Alto coast, [Excellence Punta Cana](/resorts/excellence-punta-cana/) offers swim-up suites and a calm atmosphere with good value for what's included.

### Sanctuary Cap Cana

In the upscale Cap Cana enclave, [Sanctuary Cap Cana](/resorts/sanctuary-cap-cana/) offers a peaceful, more exclusive setting close to the airport.

### Hammock Cove

In Antigua, [Hammock Cove](/resorts/hammock-cove/) is a boutique adults-only all-inclusive where every waterfront villa has a private plunge pool.

## Best adults-only resorts in the United States

### Hotel Wailea, Maui

Set on the hills above Wailea, [Hotel Wailea](/resorts/hotel-wailea/) is an intimate, all-suite hotel with ocean views and a serene pool. It's one of Hawaii's few adults-only hotels.

### Post Ranch Inn, Big Sur

Perched on cliffs above the Pacific, [Post Ranch Inn](/resorts/post-ranch-inn/) blends architecture into the landscape. Its views and hush make it one of America's most romantic hotels.

### Ventana Big Sur

Among redwoods and meadows, [Ventana Big Sur](/resorts/ventana-big-sur/) offers suites and luxury glamping with Pacific views at dinner.

### Little Palm Island, Florida Keys

Arrive by boat or seaplane at [Little Palm Island](/resorts/little-palm-island/), an adults-only private island with thatched-roof bungalows and dinners by the water.

## Luxury vs. affordable adults-only resorts

**Luxury adults-only resorts** focus on setting and service: private plunge pools, butlers, clifftop views and intimate size. Examples include Hammock Cove, Le Blanc, Post Ranch Inn and Little Palm Island. See [luxury resorts for couples](/guides/luxury-resorts-for-couples/).

**Affordable adults-only resorts** are often all-inclusives in destinations with lots of competition, like Punta Cana and Cancun, especially in shoulder season. Traveling midweek and choosing a standard room category helps.

## How to choose an adults-only resort

1. **Check the age policy.** Minimum ages vary, often 16 or 18.
2. **Decide on the vibe.** Lively and social, or quiet and spa-focused?
3. **Choose the meal plan.** All-inclusive for simplicity, room-only for exploring local restaurants.
4. **Look at the beach.** Calm and swimmable, or scenic but rough?
5. **Mind the transfer.** Short transfers matter most on short trips.
6. **Pick the room with purpose.** Swim-up, oceanfront or plunge pool only if you'll use it.

## What a stay at an adults-only resort is like

Days at adults-only resorts tend to move at a slower pace. Pools are quieter, and many resorts designate certain areas as "quiet zones" for reading and napping. Activities lean toward cooking and cocktail classes, yoga, snorkeling trips, wine tastings and live music in the evening. Dinners are often more relaxed, and some resorts have dress codes for à la carte restaurants in the evening. At lively resorts, expect DJs and pool parties during the day, with themed nights after dark.

## Adults-only resorts for special occasions

**Honeymoons.** Look for rooms with a private plunge pool or swim-up access, a strong spa and a romantic dinner option on the beach. Hammock Cove, Excellence Playa Mujeres and Hotel Wailea are popular choices.

**Anniversaries.** Choose a setting with a sense of occasion, like the cliffs of Big Sur or a private island in the Florida Keys.

**A friends' getaway.** Adults-only doesn't mean couples-only. Lively resorts such as Breathless Cabo San Lucas are designed for socializing.

**A reset after a busy season.** Wellness-focused resorts and quiet all-inclusives let you unplug without planning.

## When to go

In Mexico and the Caribbean, December through April is peak season with the driest weather. Late April through early June is often the sweet spot for value. Hurricane season runs June 1 through November 30. In Hawaii, spring and fall shoulder seasons are quieter. In Big Sur, September and October often bring the clearest weather.

## Mistakes to avoid when booking an adults-only resort

1. **Assuming quiet.** Some adults-only resorts are party-focused. Read the description and recent reviews.
2. **Forgetting to check the age policy** if traveling with an older teen or young adult.
3. **Overlooking the meal plan.** Room-only resorts in Hawaii and California mean budgeting for meals.
4. **Ignoring the transfer time** on a short trip.
5. **Booking the wrong room category.** Garden-view rooms at a beach resort can feel far from the action; pay for location if it matters to you.
6. **Not reserving spa time.** Popular treatment slots fill quickly, especially at smaller resorts.
7. **Skipping the fine print.** Check resort fees, dress codes, tipping policy and cancellation terms before you pay.
