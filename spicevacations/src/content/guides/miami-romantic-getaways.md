Miami is a romantic getaway with a pulse. It's where a sunrise walk on the sand can lead to Cuban coffee in Little Havana, an afternoon in an art-filled neighborhood and a candlelit dinner followed by salsa. It's warm when much of the country is cold, it's easy to reach, and it doesn't require a passport.

This guide covers where to stay, what to do and a three-day plan for Miami romantic getaways. For more Florida escapes, see [the best romantic getaways in Florida](/guides/best-romantic-getaways-in-florida/), and for broader planning, our complete [romantic getaways](/) guide.

## Why Miami is great for couples

- **Beaches** that glow at sunrise, with warm water most of the year.
- **Food** from Cuban cafés and Peruvian ceviche to destination restaurants.
- **Art and design**, from Art Deco hotels to Wynwood's murals.
- **Music and nightlife**, from rooftop lounges to salsa clubs.
- **Easy access**, with Miami and Fort Lauderdale airports nearby.

## Where to stay in Miami as a couple

### Mid-Beach

Mid-Beach feels more grown-up than central South Beach, with oceanfront hotels and an upscale atmosphere. [Faena Hotel Miami Beach](/resorts/faena-hotel-miami-beach/) turns a weekend into an occasion, with theatrical red-and-gold interiors, an oceanfront pool, a lavish spa and evening entertainment.

**Best for:** couples who want luxury and calm, with South Beach a short ride away.

### South Beach

South Beach is iconic: Art Deco hotels in pastel colors, Ocean Drive's energy and a walkable grid of restaurants and bars. Stay a few blocks from the busiest stretches for a calmer night's sleep.

**Best for:** couples who want to walk everywhere and enjoy nightlife.

### Coconut Grove

Leafy streets, waterfront parks and a relaxed village feel. It's close to Vizcaya Museum & Gardens and has lovely outdoor dining.

**Best for:** couples who prefer a quieter, neighborhood atmosphere.

### Key Biscayne

A short drive over the causeway, Key Biscayne feels like an island escape, with calmer beaches in Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park and Crandon Park.

**Best for:** beach lovers who want quiet.

### Brickell and Downtown

Sleek towers, rooftop bars and waterfront restaurants. Convenient for dining and nightlife, but you'll drive to the beach.

## Romantic things to do in Miami

### Watch the sunrise on the beach

Miami Beach faces east, so sunrise is the magical time. The sand is quiet, the light is soft and the ocean is often calm.

### Walk the Art Deco Historic District

Stroll Ocean Drive and Collins Avenue to admire pastel facades, neon signs and curved corners. Guided walking tours explain the history.

### Explore Wynwood

Wynwood Walls and the surrounding streets are covered in large-scale murals. Browse galleries, then grab lunch or a craft beer.

### Dance in Little Havana

Walk Calle Ocho (SW 8th Street), sip Cuban coffee from a ventanita (walk-up window), watch domino players in Máximo Gómez Park and dance to live Latin music in the evening.

### Visit Vizcaya Museum & Gardens

This Italian Renaissance-style estate on Biscayne Bay has formal gardens, fountains and bay views that feel made for strolling together.

### Take to the water

Rent paddleboards, take a sunset cruise on Biscayne Bay or charter a boat to see the skyline from the water.

### Book a spa day

Many Miami Beach hotels have excellent spas. A couples massage followed by pool time is a perfect afternoon.

### Day trip to the Keys

Drive to Key Largo in about an hour to snorkel at John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park, or continue further south for a longer adventure.

## Where to eat

Miami's food scene is one of its biggest draws:

- **Cuban classics** in Little Havana: sandwiches, croquetas and cafecito.
- **Fresh seafood** with bay or ocean views.
- **Latin American flavors**, from Peruvian ceviche to Argentine steak.
- **Design District and Brickell** for upscale, special-occasion dining.
- **Coconut Grove** for relaxed outdoor meals.

Book popular restaurants in advance, especially on weekends.

## Miami nightlife for couples

- **Rooftop bars** for skyline and ocean views at sunset.
- **Live Latin music** in Little Havana.
- **Speakeasy-style cocktail bars** in South Beach and Brickell.
- **Jazz and lounge venues** for a quieter evening.

## A three-day Miami itinerary for couples

### Day 1: Beach and Art Deco

Check in and head to the beach. Walk the Art Deco district in the late afternoon, then have dinner in South Beach and drinks at a rooftop bar.

### Day 2: Art, culture and music

Start with sunrise on the beach. Explore Wynwood's murals and galleries in the morning, then visit Vizcaya Museum & Gardens in the afternoon. In the evening, dine in Little Havana and dance to live music.

### Day 3: Island time

Drive to Key Biscayne for a beach morning and a walk to the Cape Florida Lighthouse. Return for a spa afternoon and a special farewell dinner.

## Adding the Florida Keys

With five or more days, combine Miami with the Florida Keys. Spend three nights in Miami, then drive the Overseas Highway to Islamorada or Key West for two or three nights of sunsets and snorkeling. See [Florida weekend getaways](/guides/florida-weekend-getaways/).

## When to visit Miami

| Season | Weather | Crowds and prices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| December–April | Warm and dry | Peak season, higher demand |
| May | Warming, occasional rain | Shoulder season |
| June–September | Hot, humid, afternoon storms | Often lower rates |
| October–November | Warm, drying out | Shoulder season |

Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30.

## Getting around

Rideshares are the easiest way to move between neighborhoods. In South Beach, you can walk or rent bikes. If you're day-tripping to the Keys, rent a car for that day. Parking on Miami Beach can be expensive, so factor it into your budget.

## Tips for a romantic Miami trip

- **Stay near the beach** if the ocean is your priority.
- **Plan around the heat**, with outdoor activities in the morning and evening.
- **Reserve dinners** at popular spots ahead of time.
- **Dress up a little** for evenings out; Miami nightlife is stylish.
- **Protect your skin**; the sun is strong year-round.

## Beyond Miami: nearby romantic escapes

If you'd like a quieter base or a second stop, consider Palm Beach, about an hour and a half north, with [The Breakers](/resorts/the-breakers-palm-beach/) and its elegant shopping streets, or Key West, where [Casa Marina](/resorts/casa-marina-key-west/) offers a beach resort in a historic setting.

## Romantic Miami experiences to book ahead

- **A sunset cruise** on Biscayne Bay, with skyline views as the city lights up.
- **A couples massage** at a Miami Beach spa.
- **A table at a popular restaurant** on Friday or Saturday night.
- **A guided Art Deco walking tour** in South Beach.
- **A Wynwood food or street-art tour.**
- **A salsa lesson** before a night of live music in Little Havana.

## Miami on a budget

Miami can be expensive, but many of its best experiences are free: the beach, the Art Deco district, Wynwood's murals, Little Havana's streets and sunset from South Pointe Park. Visit in late spring or early fall for lower hotel rates, eat at Cuban cafés and food halls, and use rideshares instead of renting a car.

## What to pack

Pack light, breathable clothing, swimwear, sandals and comfortable walking shoes, plus a couple of stylish outfits for dinners and nights out. Bring sunscreen, sunglasses and a light rain jacket for summer storms.

## Miami for anniversaries and special occasions

For a milestone, book an oceanfront suite, reserve a sunset dinner with bay views and add a private boat charter to see the city from the water. Many Miami hotels can arrange flowers or a celebration cake if you let them know in advance. Pair the city with a night in the Keys for a trip that combines energy and quiet.

## Miami with a view

The city is beautiful from above and from the water. Head to a rooftop bar at sunset, take a ferry or water taxi across Biscayne Bay, or walk out along the South Pointe Park pier to watch cruise ships sail past as the sky turns pink.

## Safety and practical tips

Keep valuables secure on the beach, use hotel safes, and choose licensed taxis or rideshares at night. Drink plenty of water in the heat, and check beach flags before swimming, especially when winds pick up.

## Final tip

Balance energy with rest. Miami is at its most romantic when you mix a lively night out with a slow morning on the beach, a long lunch and an afternoon nap before dinner. That rhythm lets you enjoy both sides of the city without feeling rushed or worn out.
