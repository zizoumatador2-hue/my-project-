The Waldorf Astoria Las Vegas is the Strip without the noise. There is no casino, and the arrival lobby is on the 23rd floor, with views over the city.

## Why couples love it

It is calm, polished and private, yet within walking distance of shows, restaurants and the energy of the Strip.

## Who it suits

Couples who want Vegas nights and peaceful mornings.
