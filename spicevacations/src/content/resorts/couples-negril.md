Couples Negril sits on Bloody Bay, a sheltered crescent just north of Negril's famous Seven Mile Beach. As the name suggests, it is designed exclusively for couples, and the atmosphere is laid-back and friendly.

## Why couples love it

The calm water is great for swimming and paddleboarding, and the resort's size keeps it intimate. It is a short drive to the West End cliffs for sunset.

## Who it suits

Couples looking for an unpretentious, all-inclusive beach week with a warm Jamaican welcome.
