Little Palm Island feels like a far-flung South Pacific hideaway, yet it sits in the Lower Florida Keys. Guests arrive by boat or seaplane, and the island's bungalows are tucked among palms along the water.

## Why couples love it

The sense of arrival, the privacy and the barefoot luxury make it one of Florida's most romantic resorts.

## Who it suits

Honeymooners and couples celebrating a milestone who want total seclusion without a passport.
