Breathless Cabo San Lucas is built for couples and friends who want energy with their ocean views. It sits on Medano Beach, one of Cabo San Lucas's most swimmable beaches, a short walk from the marina's boats, bars and restaurants.

## Why couples love it

The atmosphere is lively, with music, pool parties and a rooftop pool looking toward Land's End and El Arco. Being walkable to town is rare for an all-inclusive in Los Cabos.

## Who it suits

Social couples who want a fun, adults-only base. If you want total quiet, look at resorts in San José del Cabo or along the Tourist Corridor instead.
