The Don CeSar has been a Gulf Coast landmark since 1928. Its pink facade is instantly recognizable, and sunsets over the Gulf from its beach are spectacular.

## Why couples love it

Historic glamour, a wide sandy beach and sunset views make it an easy, romantic weekend base.

## Who it suits

Couples looking for a classic beach weekend near Tampa and St. Petersburg.
