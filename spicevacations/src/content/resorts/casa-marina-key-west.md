Casa Marina was built in the 1920s at the end of Henry Flagler's Overseas Railroad, and it remains one of Key West's grandest resorts, with a generous private beach rare on the island.

## Why couples love it

It combines beach-resort space with easy access to Key West's restaurants, bars and famous sunset celebration.

## Who it suits

Couples who want a beach base in Key West rather than a guesthouse in town.
