Mohonk Mountain House has welcomed guests since the 1800s. The rambling Victorian castle sits on a glacial lake high in the Shawangunk Ridge, surrounded by trails, gardens and rustic gazebos.

## Why couples love it

It feels like stepping back in time, with fireside lounges, lake views and hikes to scenic overlooks, all within an easy drive from the city.

## Who it suits

Couples wanting a classic mountain weekend from New York.
