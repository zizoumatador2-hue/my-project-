Auberge du Soleil has been a Napa Valley favorite for decades. From its hillside in Rutherford, the whole valley spreads out below, a patchwork of vineyards glowing in late-afternoon light.

## Why couples love it

The terrace restaurant at sunset, a spa built for unwinding and rooms with private terraces make it ideal for anniversaries.

## Who it suits

Food and wine lovers who want to be at the heart of Napa Valley.


## Our take

Auberge du Soleil is the stay we suggest when a couple wants Napa to feel like a celebration rather than a tasting marathon. The hillside position is the point: you look across the valley floor instead of at a parking lot, and the terrace turns an ordinary dinner into the night you remember. Book it for an anniversary or a proposal weekend, and build your days around a few unhurried winery visits rather than a packed itinerary.

## Skip it if…

- You want a lively nightlife scene; evenings in Rutherford are quiet.
- Wine is not a big part of your plans and the price premium would go unused.
- You prefer to walk to restaurants and shops from your door.

## Planning tips

- Reserve terrace dinner for golden hour, then book tastings for late morning so the drive back is easy.
- Weekdays in late winter and early spring are calmer than harvest season (roughly August to October).
- Arrange a driver or car service on tasting days instead of taking turns behind the wheel.
