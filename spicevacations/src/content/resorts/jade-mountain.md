Jade Mountain is one of the Caribbean's most distinctive resorts. Each "sanctuary" is designed without a fourth wall, so your room opens straight onto the view of the Pitons and the sea, and many include a private infinity pool.

## Why couples love it

Waking up to that open-air view is unforgettable, and the resort is built for privacy. Guests can use the beach and facilities of neighboring Anse Chastanet below.

## Who it suits

Honeymooners and couples marking a milestone who value views and seclusion above all else.
