Sanctuary Cap Cana is set within Cap Cana, an upscale, gated development known for its marina, golf and quieter atmosphere compared with the Bávaro strip.

## Why couples love it

The setting feels exclusive and calm, with coves and beaches to explore and a short transfer from the airport.

## Who it suits

Couples who want an adults-only all-inclusive with a more upscale, peaceful setting.
