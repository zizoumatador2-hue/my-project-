Couples Swept Away sits on Negril's Seven Mile Beach, surrounded by tropical gardens. It is known for its active side, with sports facilities and fitness options, balanced by a spa and plenty of quiet sand.

## Why couples love it

The beach is long and sunset-facing, the grounds feel lush and spacious, and there is plenty to do if you get restless.

## Who it suits

Active couples who like a morning workout or tennis match before a lazy beach afternoon.
