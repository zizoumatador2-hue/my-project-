Sensei Lanai is a different kind of Hawaii escape. Instead of the beach, it sits in Lanai's cooler, pine-covered uplands and focuses on rest, movement and nourishment.

## Why couples love it

The spa experiences are private and restorative, and the grounds are peaceful and filled with art. It is ideal for couples who want to reset.

## Who it suits

Couples looking for wellness and quiet rather than a traditional beach resort.
