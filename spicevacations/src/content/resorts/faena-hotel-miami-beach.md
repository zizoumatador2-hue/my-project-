Faena Hotel Miami Beach is pure theater. The interiors, created with film director Baz Luhrmann and costume designer Catherine Martin, are a riot of red, gold and art.

## Why couples love it

It turns a Miami weekend into an occasion, with glamorous dining, a lavish spa and oceanfront lounging.

## Who it suits

Couples who love design, nightlife and a sense of drama.
