Four Seasons Resort Maui at Wailea is one of Hawaii's most admired resorts, set on Wailea Beach on Maui's sunny south shore.

## Why couples love it

The adults-only Serenity Pool, with cabanas and ocean views, is a favorite for honeymooners. Service is exceptional, and the open-air lobby frames the ocean beautifully.

## Who it suits

Couples who want polished, full-service luxury right on the beach.
