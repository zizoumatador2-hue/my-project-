The Breakers is one of Florida's most famous resorts. The original hotel was founded by railroad magnate Henry Flagler in the 1890s, and the current Italian Renaissance-style building dates to the 1920s.

## Why couples love it

Grand architecture, an oceanfront setting, a large spa and a beach club make it a classic choice for anniversaries and splurge weekends.

## Who it suits

Couples who love old-world glamour and full-service luxury.
