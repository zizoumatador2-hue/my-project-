Excellence Punta Cana brings the Excellence Collection's adults-only formula to the Dominican Republic. Its Uvero Alto location is quieter than the Bávaro strip, with a long beach and a relaxed pace.

## Why couples love it

Swim-up suites, plentiful dining and a calm, grown-up atmosphere make it a strong value choice for an adults-only week.

## Who it suits

Couples who prefer peace over nightlife and do not mind a longer airport transfer.
