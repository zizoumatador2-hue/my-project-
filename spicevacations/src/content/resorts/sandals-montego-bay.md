Sandals Montego Bay is one of the brand's longest-running resorts and is close to Sangster International Airport, so you can be on the beach soon after landing.

## Why couples love it

The short transfer, the long beach and the classic Sandals formula of couples-only, all-inclusive with many dining choices make it a stress-free pick, especially for shorter trips.

## Who it suits

Couples who want to maximize beach time on a four- or five-night trip.
