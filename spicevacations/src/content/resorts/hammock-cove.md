Hammock Cove combines two things couples often have to choose between: the ease of all-inclusive and the privacy of a luxury villa. Every villa faces the water and has a private plunge pool.

## Why couples love it

It is intimate, adults-only and designed around privacy, while dining and drinks are included.

## Who it suits

Honeymooners and couples who want boutique luxury without watching the bill.
