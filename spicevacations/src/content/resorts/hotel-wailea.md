Hotel Wailea is one of Hawaii's few adults-only hotels, and it leans fully into romance. The all-suite property sits on the hills above Wailea, looking out across the ocean toward neighboring islands.

## Why couples love it

Quiet is guaranteed, the suites are spacious, and sunset from the hillside is spectacular. Wailea's beaches, shops and restaurants are a short drive away.

## Who it suits

Honeymooners and couples who prefer an intimate boutique hotel over a large beach resort.

Read our [Hawaii couples vacation](/guides/hawaii-couples-vacation/) guide.

## Our take

Hotel Wailea is our pick for couples who want Maui romance on a smaller, quieter scale. The all-suite, adults-only hotel sits on a hillside above the coast, so evenings feel private and dining is intimate. A shuttle to the Wailea beaches means you still get the ocean, just without a crowded resort pool scene.

## Skip it if…

- You want to step straight out of your room onto the sand.
- You want an all-inclusive or a large resort with many amenities.
- You are traveling with children.

## Planning tips

- Rent a car to explore Upcountry Maui and the Road to Hana.
- Book the hotel restaurant for a sunset table.
- Kahului (OGG) is the closest airport, about 30–40 minutes away.
