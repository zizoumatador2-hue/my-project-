Halekulani has long been one of Waikiki's most refined hotels. Its name is often translated as "house befitting heaven," and the atmosphere is serene despite the busy beach outside.

## Why couples love it

Evenings at House Without a Key, with sunset, live Hawaiian music and hula under a kiawe tree, are a Waikiki tradition. The pool, with its orchid mosaic, is one of the most photographed in Hawaii.

## Who it suits

Couples who want Honolulu's dining and culture with a tranquil, classic base.
