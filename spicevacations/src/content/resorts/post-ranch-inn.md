Post Ranch Inn is one of America's most romantic hotels. Set on the ridge above the Pacific in Big Sur, its rooms, some shaped like treehouses and others built into the hillside, are designed to disappear into the landscape.

## Why couples love it

The views are extraordinary, the atmosphere is hushed and adults-only, and the restaurant's windows frame the ocean.

## Who it suits

Couples celebrating something big who want nature and luxury in equal measure.


## Our take

Post Ranch Inn is Big Sur at its most romantic: adults-only, clifftop rooms with sweeping Pacific views and architecture that blends into the land. The infinity pool and destination restaurant make it easy to stay put. Choose it for a special-occasion splurge when the scenery is the main event.

## Skip it if…

- You want a beach you can walk onto.
- You need reliable phone service and nightlife.
- You are traveling on a budget.

## Planning tips

- Check Highway 1 conditions before your trip; closures happen after storms.
- Fill up on gas before entering Big Sur; stations are few and expensive.
- Book dinner reservations at the time you book your room.
