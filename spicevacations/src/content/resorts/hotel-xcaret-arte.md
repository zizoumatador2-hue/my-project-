Hotel Xcaret Arte is a different kind of adults-only resort. Rather than generic luxury, it celebrates Mexico: its architecture, food, music and craft traditions are woven into the design, the restaurants and the activities.

## Why couples love it

Beyond the pools and spa, guests can join workshops in areas like painting, dance or cooking, which makes it a good fit for couples who want to do more than lie by the pool. Its sister parks nearby, including Xcaret and Xel-Há, add adventure days.

## Who it suits

Culture-curious couples and honeymooners who want an all-inclusive with personality.
