## How much does a romantic getaway cost?

The honest answer is "it depends," and anyone who quotes a single price without knowing your dates is guessing. Costs change with season, day of the week, how far ahead you book and what room you choose. What we can give you is a reliable way to plan.

### Build your budget in four parts

1. **Getting there.** Flights or gas, plus airport parking and transfers.
2. **Where you sleep.** Usually the biggest line item, and the one that swings most by season.
3. **Food and drink.** Nothing to add at an all-inclusive, beyond tips and extras; a significant daily cost if you stay room-only.
4. **Experiences and extras.** Tours, spa treatments, shows, tips and souvenirs.

Once you know your total, work backward. If lodging takes too much of it, shorten the stay, choose a shoulder-season date or pick a destination closer to home. Our [Plan My Vacation](/plan-my-vacation/) tool turns your total budget into a rough per-person, per-day style (value, mid-range or splurge) and suggests matching places.

### Where to save and where to splurge

- **Save** on flights by staying flexible by a few days, and on lodging by traveling midweek or in shoulder season.
- **Splurge** on the room if you'll spend time in it (a balcony or plunge pool) and on one unforgettable experience.
- **Don't skimp** on location. A cheaper hotel an hour from everything costs you time, which is the one thing a romantic getaway can't replace.

## Affordable romantic getaways

A romantic getaway doesn't have to be expensive. Some of the most memorable trips are simple: a two-night stay in a beach town, a cabin by a lake, or a city weekend focused on long walks and one great dinner.

Good-value ideas include Florida's Gulf Coast outside of peak winter weeks, midweek Las Vegas, Mexico's Riviera Maya in shoulder season and drive-to escapes in your own region. ## How to find real vacation deals

Real deals come from timing and flexibility, not countdown timers. The patterns that matter most:

- **Shoulder seasons** between peak and low season.
- **Midweek stays** in weekend-heavy destinations like Las Vegas and the Florida Keys.
- **Longer-stay offers** at some resorts, which change often, so always check live terms.
- **Booking windows**: popular resorts and holiday dates sell out early; flexible, off-peak trips can be booked closer in.

## Making any trip more romantic

Great destinations help, but small choices make the memories:

- **Plan one surprise.** A sunset sail, breakfast delivered to your balcony or a handwritten note in a suitcase.
- **Protect the first evening.** Book an easy dinner near your hotel so arrival day ends on a high note.
- **Go analog.** Agree on phone-free dinners.
- **Chase golden hour.** Plan walks, drives and photos around sunrise and sunset.
- **Try something new together.** A cooking class, a snorkel trip or a hike you've never done builds a shared story.
- **Tell the hotel why you're there.** Mentioning an anniversary or honeymoon when you book is free and sometimes leads to thoughtful touches, though it's never guaranteed.

## Common mistakes to avoid

1. **Overscheduling.** Two activities a day is plenty.
2. **Ignoring transfer times.** A 90-minute drive from the airport can eat half a day on a short trip.
3. **Booking the wrong season.** Research weather, crowds and hurricane risk for your dates.
4. **Skipping the fine print.** Check resort fees, what an all-inclusive actually includes and cancellation terms.
5. **Forgetting documents.** Check passport validity well ahead of any international trip.

## What to pack for a romantic getaway

Packing light makes travel smoother, but a few items make a trip feel special:

- **One dressy outfit each** for a celebration dinner.
- **A small speaker or shared playlist** for your balcony or room.
- **Reef-safe sunscreen, a hat and sunglasses** for beach destinations.
- **Comfortable walking shoes** for cities and scenic trails.
- **A light layer**, because restaurants, flights and desert nights can be chilly.
- **Copies of important documents**, including passports for international trips, stored separately from the originals.
- **A small surprise**, such as a card, a favorite snack or a framed photo for the nightstand.
