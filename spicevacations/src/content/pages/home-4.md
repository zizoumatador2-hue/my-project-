## Caribbean and Mexico vacations for couples

When most Americans picture a romantic getaway, they picture turquoise water. The Caribbean and Mexico deliver it within a short flight of most U.S. cities, along with the widest choice of adults-only and all-inclusive resorts anywhere.

### Mexico: the easy favorite

[Mexico](/destinations/mexico/) has two very different coastlines. The **Caribbean side**, from Cancun south through Playa del Carmen to Tulum, offers calm turquoise water, cenotes (natural swimming holes) and Mayan ruins. Just north of Cancun, Playa Mujeres is home to [Excellence Playa Mujeres](/resorts/excellence-playa-mujeres/), while [Secrets Maroma Beach](/resorts/secrets-maroma-beach/) sits on one of the Riviera Maya's most admired beaches. [Hotel Xcaret Arte](/resorts/hotel-xcaret-arte/) celebrates Mexican art and culture in an adults-only setting, and [Le Blanc Spa Resort Cancun](/resorts/le-blanc-spa-resort-cancun/) is known for butler service in the Hotel Zone.

The **Pacific side** includes Los Cabos and Puerto Vallarta. Cabo pairs desert mountains with dramatic ocean scenery and winter whale watching, though many of its beaches are too rough for swimming. Puerto Vallarta has a walkable old town and jungle-covered hills.

For a full comparison, read [Mexico vacations for couples](/guides/mexico-vacations-for-couples/) and our picks for the [best adults-only resorts in Mexico](/guides/best-adults-only-resorts-in-mexico/).

### Jamaica: couples resorts and cliffside sunsets

[Jamaica](/destinations/jamaica/) is where couples-only all-inclusives became famous. Negril's Seven Mile Beach and West End cliffs are made for sunsets, Montego Bay offers short airport transfers, and Ocho Rios is the base for waterfalls and river adventures.

### The Bahamas: closer than you think

The [Bahamas](/destinations/bahamas/) are about an hour's flight from South Florida. Stay on Nassau's Cable Beach for resorts and restaurants, or head to the Out Islands, like the Exumas and Harbour Island, for turquoise sandbars and pink-sand beaches. Short Bahamas cruises from Florida are another easy option.

### The Dominican Republic: all-inclusive value

The [Dominican Republic](/destinations/dominican-republic/) offers long, palm-lined beaches and some of the best all-inclusive value in the region. Punta Cana and Bávaro have the most resorts; quieter Uvero Alto is home to [Excellence Punta Cana](/resorts/excellence-punta-cana/), and upscale Cap Cana hosts [Sanctuary Cap Cana](/resorts/sanctuary-cap-cana/).

### St. Lucia, Antigua and the wider Caribbean

For a milestone trip, the wider [Caribbean](/destinations/caribbean/) has some truly unforgettable settings. St. Lucia's Pitons frame [Jade Mountain](/resorts/jade-mountain/) and [Sugar Beach, A Viceroy Resort](/resorts/sugar-beach-viceroy/). Antigua's [Hammock Cove](/resorts/hammock-cove/) gives every villa a private plunge pool. Turks and Caicos' Grace Bay is one of the region's most celebrated beaches, and Aruba sits south of the main hurricane belt with reliably sunny weather.

Explore more in [Caribbean vacations for couples](/guides/caribbean-vacations-for-couples/) and [the best beach vacations in the Caribbean](/guides/best-beach-vacations-in-the-caribbean/).

### When to go to the Caribbean and Mexico

- **December to April:** dry season and peak demand. Book early, especially for holidays.
- **Late April to early June:** often the sweet spot for value and weather.
- **June 1 to November 30:** Atlantic hurricane season. Many trips go smoothly, but book flexible rates and consider travel insurance. Mexico's Pacific coast has its own hurricane season that begins in mid-May.
- **Sargassum:** this seaweed can reach Mexican Caribbean and some Caribbean beaches, most often in warmer months. Levels vary year to year and beach to beach. Ask your resort about recent conditions.

### Passports, entry and safety

U.S. citizens need a passport for Mexico, the Bahamas, Jamaica, the Dominican Republic and most Caribbean islands. Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands are exceptions. Before booking any international trip, check the U.S. State Department's travel advisory for your destination, which is updated regularly and can differ by region within a country.

### Romantic things to do in the Caribbean and Mexico

Once you've arrived, a few experiences are worth planning in advance:

- **Sunset sails.** Catamaran cruises are offered in nearly every resort area and are one of the most romantic ways to end a day.
- **Snorkeling.** Calm, clear water on Mexico's Caribbean coast, in the Bahamas and around many Caribbean islands makes it easy for beginners.
- **Cenotes.** In the Yucatán Peninsula, swimming in a freshwater cenote is unforgettable. Go early to beat tour groups.
- **Waterfalls and rainforest.** Jamaica's Dunn's River Falls and St. Lucia's rainforest trails add adventure to beach days.
- **Local food.** Try jerk chicken in Jamaica, fish tacos in Baja, conch in the Bahamas and mofongo in Puerto Rico or the Dominican Republic.
- **Couples spa treatments.** Many resorts offer side-by-side massages, often with open-air or oceanview treatment rooms.

A little local exploration goes a long way, even at an all-inclusive. One day out of the resort gives your trip texture, and you'll come home with stories as well as a tan.
