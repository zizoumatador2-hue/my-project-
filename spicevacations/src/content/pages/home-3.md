## Romantic getaways in the USA

You don't need a passport for an unforgettable romantic getaway. The United States has tropical islands, desert canyons, wine country, big cities and some of the world's great coastal drives. Here's how the most popular options compare. For more detail, read our full guide to [romantic getaways in the USA](/guides/romantic-getaways-in-the-usa/).

### Florida: sunsets, islands and easy escapes

Florida is the most versatile romantic destination in the continental U.S. The [Florida Keys](/destinations/florida/) string together islands linked by the Overseas Highway, ending in Key West, where the daily sunset celebration at Mallory Square is a tradition. For seclusion, [Little Palm Island](/resorts/little-palm-island/) is an adults-only private island reached by boat or seaplane.

The Gulf Coast offers calmer water and golden sunsets. Siesta Key is known for its fine, white quartz sand, and [The Don CeSar](/resorts/the-don-cesar/) has been St. Pete Beach's pink landmark since 1928. On the Atlantic side, Palm Beach's [The Breakers](/resorts/the-breakers-palm-beach/) is pure old-world glamour.

For a complete itinerary, read [the best romantic getaways in Florida](/guides/best-romantic-getaways-in-florida/), or plan something shorter with our [Florida weekend getaways](/guides/florida-weekend-getaways/).

### Miami: beach days and Latin nights

[Miami](/destinations/miami/) combines sunrise beach walks, Art Deco architecture, Cuban coffee in Little Havana and some of the country's best restaurants. Stay in Mid-Beach for a more grown-up feel; [Faena Hotel Miami Beach](/resorts/faena-hotel-miami-beach/) turns a weekend into an occasion. Our [Miami romantic getaways](/guides/miami-romantic-getaways/) guide has a three-day plan.

### Hawaii: the no-passport honeymoon

[Hawaii](/destinations/hawaii/) feels like another world. Maui is the classic choice for couples, with resort beaches in Wailea, the Road to Hana and sunrise at Haleakala (reservations are required for sunrise entry). Kauai is wilder and quieter, the Big Island is volcanic and vast, and Oahu mixes Waikiki's energy with the North Shore's laid-back charm.

Hawaii works best with at least a week. Pick one island for trips under ten days. See our complete [Hawaii couples vacation](/guides/hawaii-couples-vacation/) guide.

### California: cliffs, vineyards and golden light

[California](/destinations/california/) delivers some of the most scenic romantic getaways in the country. Drive Highway 1 through Big Sur, taste your way through Napa and Sonoma, stroll Carmel-by-the-Sea or soak up desert style in Palm Springs. Big Sur's adults-only retreats, [Post Ranch Inn](/resorts/post-ranch-inn/) and [Ventana Big Sur](/resorts/ventana-big-sur/), are bucket-list stays. Before any Big Sur trip, check Highway 1 road conditions, since sections can close for repairs.

### Las Vegas: shows, spas and desert sunsets

[Las Vegas](/destinations/las-vegas/) is a surprisingly good couples getaway if you plan beyond the casinos. See a show, have dinner overlooking the Bellagio fountains, spend a morning at Red Rock Canyon and end with a spa afternoon. If you want calm in the middle of the Strip, the non-gaming [Waldorf Astoria Las Vegas](/resorts/waldorf-astoria-las-vegas/) is a peaceful base. Read our [Las Vegas couples vacation](/guides/las-vegas-couples-vacation/) guide.

### New York: the city and the escapes around it

[New York](/destinations/new-york/) is a romantic destination and a launchpad. In the city, walk the Brooklyn Bridge at sunrise, see a Broadway show and find a rooftop at sunset. Within a few hours you can be in the Hudson Valley, the Catskills, the Hamptons or Newport. [Mohonk Mountain House](/resorts/mohonk-mountain-house/), a Victorian castle on a mountain lake, is a New York classic. See the [best weekend getaways from New York](/guides/best-weekend-getaways-from-new-york/).

### Orlando: theme parks for grown-ups

[Orlando](/destinations/orlando/) isn't only for families. Couples can enjoy thrill rides, nighttime spectaculars and EPCOT's food festivals, then escape to Winter Park or Central Florida's crystal-clear springs. The [Four Seasons Resort Orlando](/resorts/four-seasons-orlando/) has an adults-only pool and rooftop dining with fireworks views.

### The best beach vacations in the USA

If your idea of romance is sand between your toes, you have options on three coasts and in the middle of the Pacific. Our favorites include Siesta Key and the Florida Keys, Maui's Wailea and Kaanapali beaches, Coronado in San Diego, and the Outer Banks and Cape Cod for summer. Compare them in [the best beach vacations in the USA](/guides/best-beach-vacations-in-the-usa/).

### Weekend getaways for couples across the U.S.

The best weekend getaway is usually the one closest to home. A few favorites by region:

- **Northeast:** the Hudson Valley, Newport, Cape May, Mystic and the Berkshires.
- **Southeast:** Charleston, Savannah, the Florida Keys and St. Augustine.
- **Midwest:** Door County, Lake Geneva and Chicago for a city weekend.
- **West:** Napa, Sonoma, Santa Barbara, Palm Springs and Las Vegas.
- **Southwest:** Sedona, Santa Fe and Scottsdale.

Our guide to [weekend getaways for couples](/guides/weekend-getaways-for-couples/) turns these into easy two- and three-night plans.

### Southern charm: Charleston and Savannah

For a slower, deeply romantic weekend, the historic cities of the South are hard to beat. In Charleston, walk past the pastel houses of Rainbow Row, stroll the waterfront promenade at the Battery and plan an evening around the city's celebrated restaurants. In Savannah, Spanish moss drapes over oak-shaded squares, and Forsyth Park's fountain is a favorite spot for photos. Both cities are compact, walkable and best explored hand in hand.

### Desert romance: Sedona, Santa Fe and Palm Springs

The desert Southwest offers a different kind of romance: vast skies, red rock and starry nights. Sedona's red sandstone formations glow at sunset, and its trails and spas make for a restorative long weekend. Santa Fe's adobe architecture, art galleries and chile-spiced cuisine feel like a trip abroad. Palm Springs mixes mid-century modern design with poolside lounging, especially pleasant from fall through spring.

### Mountain escapes

Mountains slow everything down. Consider Asheville and the Blue Ridge Parkway in fall, Lake Tahoe for summer swims and winter snow, or a cabin in the Smokies. The formula is simple: a cozy place to stay, one good hike, a long dinner and a fireplace.
