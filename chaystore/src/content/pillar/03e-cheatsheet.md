## The One-Page Brewing Cheat Sheet

Bookmark this section. It condenses the essentials into a single reference you can use every day. Amounts are per 8 oz (240 ml) cup and are starting points to adjust.

| Tea | Leaf | Water | Steep | Re-steeps | Milk? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| English breakfast / Assam | 1 tsp | 212°F | 3–5 min | 1 | Yes |
| Darjeeling | 1–1½ tsp | 195–205°F | 2–4 min | 1–2 | Rarely |
| Earl Grey | 1 tsp | 212°F | 3–5 min | 1 | Optional |
| Masala chai (simmered) | 2 tsp per 2 cups | Simmer | 5 min spices + 2–3 min tea | No | Yes |
| Sencha | 1 tsp | 160–175°F | 1–2 min | 2–3 | No |
| Dragon Well | 1–1½ tsp | 170–180°F | 2–3 min | 2–3 | No |
| Jasmine green | 1 tsp | 175–185°F | 2–3 min | 2–3 | No |
| White tea | 1½–2 tsp | 175–185°F | 2–4 min | 2–3 | No |
| Oolong | 1–2 tsp | 185–205°F | 3–5 min | 3+ | No |
| Hojicha | 1 tsp | 195–205°F | 30 sec–2 min | 2 | Optional |
| Pu-erh | 1 tsp | 195–212°F | 3–5 min | Several | No |
| Matcha | ½–1 tsp | 175°F | Whisk | None | Latte: yes |
| Herbal blends | 1–2 tsp | 212°F | 5–7 min | 1 | Optional |
| Rooibos | 1–2 tsp | 212°F | 5–7 min | 1–2 | Optional |

For the reasoning behind these numbers, see [how to brew loose leaf tea](/guides/how-to-brew-loose-leaf-tea/), [how much tea per cup](/guides/how-much-tea-per-cup/) and [water temperature for green tea](/guides/water-temperature-for-green-tea/).

### If something tastes wrong

- **Bitter or dry:** cooler water, shorter steep, less leaf.
- **Weak:** more leaf, larger infuser, fresher tea.
- **Flat:** fresh water and fresher tea.
- **Too grassy or fishy (green tea):** water slightly hotter or steep slightly longer, and check freshness.
- **Too sour or sharp:** water may be too cool for black tea, or the tea is under-steeped.

## Choosing a Tea by What You Want

Sometimes you do not know a tea type; you know what you want from a cup. Use this decision guide.

| You want... | Start with... | Because... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Something familiar and strong | A breakfast blend or Assam | Bold, comforting, easy to brew |
| A gentler lift with less caffeine | Green or white tea | Typically lower in caffeine |
| A creamy, café-style drink | A [matcha latte](/guides/how-to-make-matcha-latte/) or [chai](/guides/how-to-make-chai-at-home/) | Milk-friendly and rich |
| Floral and aromatic | Jasmine green, Darjeeling | Delicate and perfumed |
| No caffeine | Rooibos, chamomile, peppermint | Naturally caffeine-free |
| A cold, refreshing drink | Cold-brewed green, white, fruit or hibiscus | Smooth with little bitterness |
| A tea to share with guests | A pot of oolong or a pot of Earl Grey | Pleasant for many palates |
| Something to explore slowly | Oolong, pu-erh, single-estate teas | Evolves over many infusions |

When in doubt, start small: buy a sample size, brew it twice and decide.
