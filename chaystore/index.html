FAQ

How should I store my tea?

Keep it in the resealable pouch or an airtight tin, away from light, heat and strong odors.

How do I brew loose-leaf tea?

Use about 1 teaspoon per 8 oz cup. Black: 212°F for 3–5 min. Green: 175°F for 2–3 min. Herbal: 212°F for 5–7 min.

Is your tea caffeine-free?

Black, green, oolong and matcha contain caffeine. Our herbal blends are naturally caffeine-free and labeled as such.
