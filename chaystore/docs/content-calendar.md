# 90-day roadmap
**Weeks 1–2: setup.** Deploy, attach domain, Search Console/Bing, real contact mailbox, images for all 22 articles, fact-check pass.
**Weeks 3–6: build depth.** Publish 3–5 new cluster articles per week from `keyword-tracker.csv` (guides first, comparisons next). Interlink every new article to its hub, the pillar and 3 related guides.
**Weeks 7–10: expand + links.** Gear/buying guides with affiliate boxes (after Associates approval); outreach for a handful of relevant links (food/health blogs, tea communities); review rankings in Search Console and improve titles with low CTR.
**Weeks 11–13: refine.** Refresh top pages, add FAQ/table snippets where you have impressions but no clicks, internal-link audit, decide on ad network application once ~30 strong articles and steady traffic exist.

Metrics to log monthly in `metrics.csv`: sessions, organic clicks, impressions, avg position, RPM, affiliate revenue.
